## ⚡ Golden Speedrun Rules

1. **Only craft items that advance tier gating or boss DPS.** No cosmetic farming, no decorative building, no vanity slots.
2. **Move fast, dig deep.** Day 1 surface chest sweep → bomb straight to cavern layer for life crystals and gold chests.
3. **Keep essentials only:** Hermes/Sailfish/Flurry boots, Cloud/Blizzard/Sandstorm in a Bottle, Magic Mirror/Recall potions, Grappling Hook, Band of Regen, Copper Shortsword (save in chest for Zenith later).
4. **Do not fish or farm achievements.** Zero Angler quests, zero pet hunting, zero golf, zero biome cleansing.
5. **NPC Housing: Minimum Viable Camp.** 3–4 basic rectangular wooden rooms for Merchant, Nurse, Arms Dealer, and Goblin Tinkerer.

## Phase 1 — Fast Drop & Essential Mobility

**Goal:** Get running boots, double jump, grappling hook, 200 HP, and basic housing.

### 1. Minimal Camp Setup
- [ ] Chop trees for ~150 wood. Place Work Bench, Wooden Hammer, Wooden Sword & Bow.
- [ ] Build 3–4 standard valid houses (~10×6 interior, player-placed wood background walls, door, chair, table, torch).
- [ ] Move Guide in; open rooms for Merchant (50 silver) and Nurse.

### 2. Surface Sweep & Cavern Dive
- [ ] Run left/right across surface: loot surface chests (spear, Wand of Sparking, recall potions, grenades/bombs).
- [ ] Loot Living Trees (Finch Staff / Aglet / Climbing Claws).
- [ ] Bomb/dig straight down to Caverns:
  - [ ] Gather 20 Stone (Furnace) + 10 Iron/Lead ore (Anvil).
  - [ ] Craft **Iron / Lead Pickaxe**.
  - [ ] Loot Underground Cabins: get **Hermes / Flurry / Sailfish Boots**, **Cloud / Blizzard in a Bottle**, **Magic Mirror**.
  - [ ] Mine 15 of any single Gem type (Ruby, Diamond, Amethyst, Emerald, Sapphire, Topaz) → craft **Gem Grappling Hook**.
  - [ ] Smash Life Crystals immediately until reaching **200+ HP**.

> **Phase 1 Exit Checklist:** Boots equipped + Double jump bottle + Hook + Magic Mirror + 200+ HP.

## Phase 2 — Evil Boss Rush & Hellstone Rush

**Goal:** Break orbs/hearts, kill Eater of Worlds / Brain of Cthulhu, forge the pickaxe, and mine Hellstone.

### 1. Evil Chasms
- [ ] Buy bombs from Demolitionist (or craft sticky bombs).
- [ ] Head to Corruption or Crimson chasm.
- [ ] Blow up 2 Orbs / Hearts (drops first gun → spawns Arms Dealer + Musket/Undertaker).
- [ ] Blow up the 3rd Orb / Heart to summon **Eater of Worlds** or **Brain of Cthulhu**:
  - *EoW Strategy:* Piercing weapons (Grenades, Vilethorn, Jester Arrows, Ball O' Hurt).
  - *BoC Strategy:* Grenades / Musket / Rotten Eggs for Creepers; knockback sword/spear for phase 2.
- [ ] Collect Shadow Scales / Tissue Samples + Demonite / Crimtane Bars.
- [ ] Craft **Nightmare Pickaxe** or **Deathbringer Pickaxe** *(Crucial speedrun gate: unlocks Hellstone mining)*.

### 2. (Optional) Fast Eye of Cthulhu / Queen Bee
- [ ] **Eye of Cthulhu:** Kill if summoned naturally or pop Suspicious Looking Eye (grab Shield of Cthulhu dash on Expert).
- [ ] **Queen Bee (Recommended for speed):** Find a hive in Jungle, defeat Queen Bee 1–2 times for Beenades (melts Skeletron and Wall of Flesh).

### 3. Underworld & Molten Gear
- [ ] Dig hellevator down to the Underworld (Hell).
- [ ] Mine Hellstone and Obsidian (use water + lava).
- [ ] Loot a **Hellforge** from a ruined building.
- [ ] Smelt Hellstone Bars:
  - [ ] Craft **Molten Pickaxe** *(mines Hardmode ores)*.
  - [ ] Craft **Molten Armor** (or class set) & **Molten Fury** / **Volcano** / **Flamarang**.

> **Phase 2 Exit Checklist:** Nightmare/Deathbringer Pickaxe + Molten Pickaxe + solid high-damage weapons/Beenades.

## Phase 3 — Skeletron & Wall of Flesh (Pre-Hardmode Complete)

**Goal:** Unlock Dungeon, build the Hell Bridge, and defeat Wall of Flesh.

### 1. Skeletron & Dungeon Sweep
- [ ] Head to Dungeon at night, speak to Old Man, defeat **Skeletron** (circle with boots/double jump, use Beenades / Minishark / Molten Fury).
- [ ] Quick Dungeon raid:
  - [ ] Grab **Cobalt Shield** (prevents knockback — mandatory for speed and survivability).
  - [ ] Grab **Shadow Key** and **Muramasa** (for Night's Edge).
  - [ ] Free the **Mechanic** and **Goblin Tinkerer** (if found bound). Combine Boots + Rocket Boots at Tinkerer's Workshop if available.

### 2. Wall of Flesh Execution
- [ ] Build a long, flat Underworld runway (~1000–1500 blocks of dirt/stone/wood platforms, cleared of roof obstacles).
- [ ] Obtain a **Guide Voodoo Doll** (kill Voodoo Demon over solid ground).
- [ ] Ensure Guide is alive. Drink Ironskin, Swiftness, and Regeneration potions.
- [ ] Throw doll into lava.
- [ ] **Defeat Wall of Flesh:** Run backwards down the bridge spamming Beenades, Minishark (Meteor/Silver bullets), Molten Fury (Hellfire arrows), or Water Bolt.
- [ ] Collect Expert Treasure Bag: **Demon Heart** (6th accessory slot) + **Class Emblem** (Warrior / Ranger / Sorcerer / Summoner).

> **Phase 3 Exit Checklist:** Pwnhammer in inventory, Hardmode triggered, 6th accessory slot consumed.

## Phase 4 — Early Hardmode Blitz

**Goal:** Rapidly bypass early Hardmode danger and build boss-killing loadouts.

- [ ] Take Pwnhammer and smash **3 to 6 Demon/Crimson Altars** (spawns Cobalt/Palladium, Mythril/Orichalcum, Titanium/Adamantite). *Do not smash more to avoid excess evil spread.*
- [ ] Equip Molten Pickaxe and rush underground:
  - [ ] Mine tier 1 ore (Cobalt/Palladium) → craft Pickaxe/Drill.
  - [ ] Mine tier 2 ore (Mythril/Orichalcum) → craft **Mythril/Orichalcum Anvil**.
  - [ ] Mine tier 3 ore (Titanium/Adamantite) → craft **Titanium/Adamantite Forge** + full **Titanium/Adamantite Armor**.
- [ ] **Wings Rush:** Kill Wyverns in Space for Souls of Flight (20) + Harpy Feather / buy Leaf Wings from Witch Doctor in Jungle at night.
- [ ] **Pick Primary Hardmode Weapon:**
  - *Ranger:* Daedalus Stormbow (from Hallowed Mimic using Key of Light) + Holy Arrows, or Onyx Blaster / Megashark.
  - *Melee:* Shadowflame Knife (Goblin Summoner), Amarok / Yelets yoyo + Yoyo Bag, or Fetid Baghnakhs (Crimson Mimic).
  - *Mage:* Crystal Serpent (quick water pool) / Golden Shower (Ichor) / Spirit Flame.
  - *Summoner:* Spider Armor + Spider Staff / Sanguine Staff.

> **Phase 4 Exit Checklist:** Full Tier 3 HM Armor (or class equivalent) + Hardmode Wings + High DPS Boss Weapon.

## Phase 5 — The Mechanical Bosses

**Goal:** Defeat Destroyer, Twins, and Skeletron Prime; unlock Chlorophyte and Hallowed gear.

- [ ] **1. The Destroyer:**
  - Build a high sky platform (~40–50 blocks up to stay out of head reach).
  - Rain down Daedalus Stormbow + Holy Arrows / Nimbus Rod / Tome of Infinite Wisdom / Piercing melee.
  - Collect Souls of Might.
- [ ] **2. The Twins (Retinazer & Spazmatism):**
  - Long asphalt or wooden runway.
  - Focus down Spazmatism (green flame) first while backing away, then kill Retinazer.
  - Collect Souls of Sight.
- [ ] **3. Skeletron Prime:**
  - Circular arena with campfires/heart lanterns. Target the head or destroy the laser/cannon arms first.
  - Collect Souls of Fright.
- [ ] **Craft Progression Milestones:**
  - [ ] Combine Souls of Sight + Might + Fright + Hallowed Bars → **Pickaxe Axe** or **Drax**.
  - [ ] Craft **Hallowed Armor** (Holy Protection dodge buff).
  - [ ] (Optional) Craft **True Night's Edge** & **True Excalibur**.

> **Phase 5 Exit Checklist:** All 3 Mechanical Bosses defeated + Pickaxe Axe / Drax crafted ("The jungle grows restless...").

## Phase 6 — Jungle to Golem

**Goal:** Secure Chlorophyte/Life Fruit, defeat Plantera, raid the Temple, and crush Golem.

- [ ] Mine **Chlorophyte Ore** in Underground Jungle.
- [ ] Pick **Life Fruit** to reach 500 max HP (Golden Hearts).
- [ ] Craft end-tier pre-Cultist gear:
  - *Melee:* Turtle Armor → prepares for Beetle. True Excalibur / Terra Blade.
  - *Ranger:* Shroomite Armor + Megashark / Chlorophyte Shotbow.
  - *Mage:* Spectre Armor + Magnet Sphere / Rainbow Gun / Razorpine.
  - *Summoner:* Spooky Armor / Tiki Armor + Blade Staff / Raven Staff.
- [ ] **Plantera:**
  - Clear out a large rectangular box arena in the underground jungle with platforms.
  - Pop a Plantera Bulb. Circle around her second phase using wings and dash.
  - Collect **Temple Key** and weapon drop.
- [ ] **Lihzahrd Temple & Golem:**
  - Unlock Jungle Temple door with Temple Key.
  - Avoid/disarm dart and spiky ball wire traps.
  - Reach the altar chamber. Place Lihzahrd Power Cells to spawn **Golem**.
  - Defeat Golem (high platform over head makes lasers miss).
  - Craft **Beetle Armor** (Melee) or utilize Golem drops (Eye of the Golem, Beetle husks, Possessed Hatchet).

> **Phase 6 Exit Checklist:** Golem dead + Beetle/Shroomite/Spectre/Spooky Tier Armor equipped.

## Phase 7 — Lunar Events & Moon Lord (Final Boss)

**Goal:** Slay the Lunatic Cultist, clear the 4 Pillars, and beat the game.

### 1. Lunatic Cultist
- [ ] Head to the Dungeon entrance.
- [ ] Slay the 4 cultists to trigger **Lunatic Cultist**.
- [ ] Focus attacks on the real cultist (avoid striking illusions during the spell circle).

### 2. The 4 Celestial Pillars
*Clear shields by killing 100 enemies per pillar, then destroy the pillar core:*
- [ ] **Solar Pillar (Melee):** Stay on the ground (Crawltipedes only target airborne players!). Craft **Solar Eruption** or **Daybreak**.
- [ ] **Vortex Pillar (Ranged):** Keep moving, craft **Vortex Beater** / **Phantasm**.
- [ ] **Nebula Pillar (Mage):** Dodge teleporting brain suckers, craft **Nebula Arcanum** / **Nebula Blaze**.
- [ ] **Stardust Pillar (Summoner):** Craft **Stardust Dragon Staff** or **Stardust Cell Staff**.

### 3. Moon Lord Boss Fight
- [ ] Construct a long sky bridge (or Asphalt runway) with nurse station / honey pools / campfires.
- [ ] **When Moon Lord spawns (~1 minute after 4th pillar):**
  - Focus and destroy the Phantasmal True Eye on the Forehead first (jump over the giant Phantasmal Deathray with rod of discord, high flight, or dash).
  - Destroy both Hand Eyes.
  - Focus down the Core in the chest when it opens.
- [ ] **Kill Moon Lord → THE GAME IS BEATEN! 🎉**
