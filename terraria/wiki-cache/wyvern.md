# Wyvern

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wyvern
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:10:54.696Z

---

The Wyvern is a large Hardmode enemy that spawns in Space. It resembles a long, white Chinese dragon and follows the Worm AI.
It will follow descending players down to ground level and can fly through blocks, but regular Wyverns tend not to fly below ground.
Wyverns will only spawn when the player is above a certain altitude (see Space altitudes) and is outside of the Dungeon biome. Furthermore, Wyverns have additional spawn conditions.


== Segments ==
The Wyvern consists of 15 segments in total. The first segment is the head, the third and tenth are the legs, the second, fourth through eighth, eleventh and twelfth are body segments, and the last three are the tail segments.
