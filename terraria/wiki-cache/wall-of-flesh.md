# Wall of Flesh

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh
Fetched: 2026-08-20T07:58:54.454Z

---

The Wall of Flesh is the final pre-Hardmode boss. Once it is defeated, the world permanently converts to Hardmode, which brings new content and challenges.
To defeat the Wall of Flesh, the player must attack either its eyes or its mouth. They all share the same health counter, so attacking any of the three will deplete the total health of the boss. However, the eyes have less defense (thus take more damage) than the mouth. No other parts of the Wall of Flesh can be damaged.
See below for information on the two exclusive debuffs from the boss, Horrified and The Tongue.
While the Wall of Flesh is alive, its exclusive music Boss 2 will play. When Otherworldly music is enabled, the track Wall of Flesh (Otherworldly) will play instead.


== Summoning ==
The Wall of Flesh can be summoned by dropping a Guide Voodoo Doll into lava in The Underworld while the Guide is alive, which destroys the Guide Voodoo Doll and kills the Guide in the process. Alternatively, the boss can also be summoned by letting the Guide die within lava in The Underworld. He can die by any means (the player, an enemy, or the environment), as long as he is touching lava when he dies.
The Wall of Flesh is summoned just beyond the player's screen on the side where the Guide Voodoo Doll was dropped into lava (or where the Guide was killed by lava), then moves in the direction of the player. As a general rule, it spawns at least 75 tiles away from the player.


=== Technical details ===
When the Guide Voodoo Doll is destroyed or the Guide is killed in lava, the game checks whether all players are horizontally at least 75 tiles away from the location of the doll/Guide. If they are, then the Wall of Flesh is spawned at that horizontal position. Otherwise, the game moves one tile towards the closest lateral world border and checks again, repeating this until it encounters a tile that is horizontally at least 75 tiles away from any player. It stops 20 tiles before the true world border (i.e. 21/22 tiles away from the visible world border), even if a player is still within 75 tiles at that point (e.g. standing directly against the visible world border).
After locating the horizontal spawn position, the game determines a valid vertical position. The spawning tile must not be occupied by a solid block and its liquid level must not exceed 100 (i.e. at most approx. 39% of the tile can contain liquid). Again, the game begins at the vertical position of the doll/Guide and checks if that location is valid. Then it checks one tile above that spot and one tile below it, then two tiles above and two tiles below, and so on, until finding a valid spot. If the game finds no valid spot after 1000 attempts (i.e. 1000 tiles above and below), then it reverts to the initial vertical position. Finally, the vertical limits are enforced: If the vertical position that was encountered is higher than 10 tiles below the Cavern–Underworld border, it is forcibly moved down to that position (even though the tile there was not considered valid before), and if it is lower than 80 tiles below that border (roughly the vertical center of the Underworld), it is forcibly moved up to that position (again, even though the tile at that position was not considered valid before).
The center of the Wall of Flesh's mouth appears at the determined location, and the eyes are spawned relative to it. The boss quickly aligns itself vertically with the player after spawning.


== Behavior ==
The Wall of Flesh is very tall, occupying the entire screen vertically. It slowly sweeps across the Underworld in a single direction, forcing the player to constantly move away for the duration of the fight while also contending with the Underworld's usual hazards.

The Wall of Flesh moves faster as its health depletes; see below for details. It can reach up to 17 mph / 41 mph at very low health levels. Due to the Wall of Flesh's higher movement speed in Expert Mode, the speed at which it draws players via the Tongue debuff in Expert also increases as its health decreases.
Additionally:

 The Wall of Flesh has small mouths called The Hungry, each attached by a long vein, which move towards and damage players. As the Wall of Flesh's main body takes damage, they deal more damage and become more resistant to attacks. When sufficiently damaged, they detach and fly towards players at high speed.
The Hungry will always drop a Heart when killed. In Expert Mode, they only have a 1/5 (20%) chance to do so, and they respawn as long as the Wall of Flesh is alive.
In Expert Mode, the Hungry gain extra knockback resistance with each additional player in multiplayer, and become immune to knockback when there are at least 5 players.
The Wall of Flesh's eyes fire purple Eye Lasers. As its health depletes, the lasers increase in damage, frequency, and velocity.
The Wall of Flesh "burps" Leeches from its mouth that chase players. These will also always / with a 1/5 (20%) chance drop a Heart when killed.
It randomly lets out a roar.

== Aftermath ==
When the Wall of Flesh is killed, it leaves its drops in a midair 9×9-tile frame of Demonite Bricks (in Corruption worlds) or Crimtane Bricks (in Crimson worlds). It will also always drop the Pwnhammer, which is required to generate Hardmode ores in the world (through breaking Demon or Crimson Altars with a hammer of 80% hammer power or higher, of which the Pwnhammer is the first obtained).

In Expert Mode or higher difficulties, its dropped Treasure Bag includes a Demon Heart, which provides the player an additional (sixth / seventh) accessory slot.
After the player has consumed a Demon Heart, additional ones will neither drop from Treasure Bags nor be consumable.
The Merchant will begin selling the Drum Set and Drumstick if he was not already.
The Angler will be able to ask for Angelfish and Cloudfish if he was not already.


=== Hardmode ===

After defeating the Wall of Flesh for the first time, the status message “The ancient spirits of light and dark have been released.” displays and the world permanently converts to Hardmode. Hardmode brings new biomes, new and tougher enemies, and a vast array of new equipment.
Two diagonal stripes of Hallow and Corruption or Crimson form in a "V" pattern from the top of the world all the way to the bottom of the Underworld. Corruption/Crimson and Hallow will spread at a much faster rate, eventually covering vital areas such as the Jungle and any NPC housing if no action is taken.


== Notes ==
Being able to summon the Wall of Flesh depends on the Guide being alive. He cannot respawn if it is nighttime or there is an event in progress. Due to this, the waiting time between multiple fights is often much greater than with other bosses. Timing the fight to finish just before dawn has a chance to allow two or even more fights, especially if the Guide already has an assigned house.
It is possible for the Guide to respawn while the Wall of Flesh is alive, although more copies of the boss will not be spawned if one is alive in the world.
When the Wall of Flesh is alive and all players in the Underworld have been killed, the screen gradually fades to black over the course of 4 seconds, after which the Wall of Flesh will despawn and the screen will return to normal visibility.
The Wall of Flesh's eyes move up and down on its body to avoid any blocks they encounter. Solid blocks and liquids 4–5 tiles above and beneath the eyes will cause them to shift their positions.
The Tongue debuff can still move players that have knockback immunity (e.g. using the Cobalt Shield or its upgrades).
In a world where the special world seed For the Worthy is active, the Wall of Flesh's eyes and mouth are much smaller, while The Hungry are much larger. Fire Imps with higher defense and faster Burning Spheres also constantly spawn throughout the fight.
As a result of the spawning mechanics, the Wall of Flesh can move towards the side of world which is laterally shorter. For example, when a Guide Voodoo Doll is dropped into lava more than 75 tiles away from the player in the right half of the world, and the player is on the right side of the location of said doll, the Wall of Flesh will be summoned on the left side of the player and move rightwards.

== Tips ==

If the Wall of Flesh is accidentally summoned, the player can quit and re-enter the world to despawn it. Note that doing so will also remove any dropped items and teleport the player back to their spawn point.
If the Wall of Flesh was recently summoned, make sure the Guide has respawned before attempting to summon the Wall of Flesh again to avoid the unnecessary loss of Voodoo Dolls.
A common "arena" to fight the boss should consist of a long horizontal bridge spanning at least 1000 blocks.
The two main types of Wall of Flesh arenas are solid bridges and platform bridges, each with their own pros and cons.
Solid bridges are made out of solid blocks, blocking lasers from the bottom eye and allowing bouncing projectiles to rebound against it, but come at the cost of blocking most of the player's own projectiles and not allowing dropping down through to evade attacks.
Platform bridges allow projectiles to pass through them, allowing the user to damage all parts of the boss more often and drop down from them if needed to dodge lasers. However, it does not block the bottom eye's lasers, and certain projectiles like Beenades do not work well unless they hit solid blocks. In Expert Mode and higher, it is recommended to build these out of Stone Platforms, Ash Wood Platforms or Obsidian Platforms which will not be destroyed by dying Lava Slimes.
If the player does not want to build a bridge, acquiring the Lava Waders or their upgrades is necessary; they can also use the Water Walking Potion.
Focus on The Hungry first before damaging the wall itself. The Hungry's damage and defense are boosted as the body takes damage, which will make them more difficult to kill if left for later.


== Trivia ==
 – According to the Terraria lore pages, the Wall of Flesh is the world's guardian. Once it is defeated, the world itself will search for a new guardian to take its place, and the Hallow is released as a temporary guardian.
The Wall of Flesh was inspired by the Demon Wall from the Japanese role-playing game series Final Fantasy.
The Hungry are the only Underworld enemy not immune to the On Fire! debuff.
The lead developer of Terraria, Redigit, has confirmed that the Guide is the Wall of Flesh on the official Terraria Discord. This explains the relevancy of his death to the appearance of the boss.
The aforementioned confirmation is consistent with the fact that the Wall of Flesh is directly referred to as male with the use of "him" in a loading screen tip.
However, the Steam Trading Cards, and even the Guide himself, say that he is instead a sacrifice to the Wall of Flesh. In addition, the Guide respawns after the Wall of Flesh is defeated.
The Wall of Flesh is one of two pre-Hardmode bosses that have at least 10,000 HP in Expert Mode, the other being Deerclops.
Although Skeletron and the Eater of Worlds can have more, they are not single-unit enemies.
The Wall of Flesh is one of the four bosses that do not make the common roar sound when summoned, the others being the Lunatic Cultist, the Queen Bee, and the Empress of Light.
The Wall of Flesh is commonly abbreviated as "WoF".
The loot's frame is always spawned at the wall's mouth, and its size is correlated with the size of the mouth. If the mouth's size changes (e.g. via external mods or the For the Worthy seed), the frame's size changes accordingly.
The Wall of Flesh is the only boss that drops a mount-summoning item in Master Mode. All other Master Mode-exclusive mount-summoning items are dropped by mini-bosses.
The veining at the back of both eye and mouth is identical to the veins trailing the Eye of Cthulhu.
The Wall of Flesh is one of the two bosses that need a town NPC to be alive before being summoned. The other is Skeletron.
In Journey Mode, if Godmode is activated, using a Magic Mirror or similar method to teleport away during the Wall of Flesh fight will not kill the player; instead, they will be dragged all the way back to the Underworld via The Tongue debuff.
The Guide's Steam Trading Card describes the Wall of Flesh as "the god of eternal nightmares".
This is, debatably, one of only two or three entities besides the player that can place blocks in the world, the others being the Antlion and Snow Balla. The Wall of Flesh only leaves behind blocks after its defeat.
