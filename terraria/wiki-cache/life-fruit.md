# Life Fruit

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Life_Fruit
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:11:51.141Z

---

The Life Fruit is a Hardmode plant and permanent booster item that grows on Jungle grass in any area within the Underground layer or below, and only after any mechanical boss has been defeated. Life Fruit grow 25% faster on average in Expert Mode.
When used, a Life Fruit permanently increases the player's health capacity by 5 and restores the player's health by 5, turning one unit in the player's health bar golden. Life Fruit can only be used once a player has reached the 400 health cap from consuming Life Crystals. A total of 20 Life Fruit is required to reach the maximum 500 health capacity. Once this is reached, the player's health cannot be increased beyond 500, with the exception of the Lifeforce buff.

== Notes ==

Life Fruit can spawn on any block of Jungle grass (including player-placed ones) below the Surface; the spawning tile does not have to be in an Underground Jungle biome, nor does it have to be off-screen. However, they will never spawn within a 122×122 (102×102) tile area (centered on the Life Fruit) of each other, i.e. they will be spaced 60 (50) tiles apart at maximum concentration.
Like other plants, Life Fruit can be harvested with any weapon or tool; a pickaxe is not required, and any attack can uproot them. They will also be uprooted if the Jungle grass beneath them is replaced.
Since they are classified as plants, Life Fruit are also affected by the Guide to Environmental Preservation and its upgrade, the Guide to Peaceful Coexistence.
Life Fruit are highlighted by the Spelunker buff or Spelunker Glowsticks by a yellow glimmer, like ores and treasures.
Life Fruit has the highest Metal Detector value. This can make searching for ores or other valuables more difficult once full health has been reached as any errant Life Fruit will be prioritized over everything else in the area.
On the minimap, Life Fruit are 2×2 squares and are a slightly lighter color of green than the rest of the foliage around it. When hovered over, they are labeled as "Life Fruit".

== Tips ==
The first Life Fruit obtained should ideally be turned into an Aegis Fruit, as 4 defense provides a greater survivability boost than 5 max health.
A Lifeforce Potion can be used to temporarily increase health capacity beyond 500, to a maximum of 600.
In order to obtain maximum health, 20 Life Fruit are required. After the player reaches it, the excess Life Fruits may be used to brew Jungle Juice.
Farms can be made by making long platforms of Jungle grass underground. Jungle grass spreads with Jungle Grass Seeds.
Life Fruit can replace all forms of wild Jungle plants that grow on Jungle grass, including Jungle Spores and the common 3-block-wide bushes, so clearing plants to encourage growth is not required. The same is true of Jungle vines and thorns. However, Life Fruit do not replace Bee Hives, so placing a row of platforms beneath each row of Jungle grass may be worthwhile to prevent them from appearing.
Life Fruit can grow in front of background walls.
Fairies can often lead the player to Life Fruit.
If the player has explored a large area of the Underground Jungle before defeating a mechanical boss, they can use the Metal Detector in conjunction with the map to find Life Fruit very effectively.
Between defeating the first and the third mechanical boss, a quick and easy way to find Life Fruit is to use the Clentaminator with Dark Blue Solution specifically, as this will break the Life Fruit and leave them on the ground, illuminated by the glowing mushroom grass for the player to see. After the player is done gathering, the biome may be easily switched back to the jungle via Green Solution. However, this method becomes much more dangerous after Plantera's Bulbs begin to spawn, because the conversion will break them and summon Plantera.


== Trivia ==
The plants on which the fruit grows have three different possible sprites. They all function identically.
The small golden heart on the Life Fruit represents the golden heart obtained when the fruit is consumed.
This item's name might be a reference to the Epic of Gilgamesh and the fruit of life Gilgamesh finds at the bottom of the sea.
