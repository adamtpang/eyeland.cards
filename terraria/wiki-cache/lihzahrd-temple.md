# Jungle Temple

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Jungle_Temple
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:11:53.153Z

---

The Jungle Temple (also known as the Lihzahrd Temple or simply The Temple) is a large, enclosed structure constructed out of Lihzahrd Bricks and located in the Underground Jungle. It is always present in any generated world, but can only be properly entered after Plantera has been defeated. The Jungle Temple contains unique Lihzahrd-themed enemies, traps, and furniture. The Lihzahrd Furnace, a crafting station for further Lihzahrd-themed furniture, can be found in its Chests. The Lihzahrd Altar found in the Temple's final chamber is used to summon the Golem boss. Defeating Golem triggers the spawning of Cultists, used to summon the Lunar Events.
The Temple is entered via the Lihzahrd Door located at one of its two top corners. The door is initially locked, requiring a Temple Key to open, which is dropped by Plantera. Unlocking the door consumes one Temple Key from the player's inventory, and the door remains permanently unlocked for all players in the world. It is generally impossible to enter the Temple via any other conventional means since Lihzahrd Bricks can only be mined with at least the Picksaw dropped by Golem, and Lihzahrd Brick Walls impose numerous restrictions, e.g. on Teleporter use. There are, however, several methods to enter the temple prior to defeating Plantera.
The Temple's entrance leads to a long corridor that winds downward through several chambers, ending in a large room at the bottom containing the Lihzahrd Altar. The Altar can be used to summon Golem at the cost of one Lihzahrd Power Cell. Lihzahrd Power Cells are guaranteed to be found in the Temple's Lihzahrd Chests and are also dropped randomly by the Temple's Lihzahrds and Flying Snakes.
The entire Temple is filled with several types of traps, including Flame, Spear, Spiky Ball, and Super Dart Traps. Traps occur much more frequently in the Temple than in the Dungeon and are far more dangerous and damaging. They are activated by Lihzahrd Pressure Plates, which only respond to players (enemies walk on them freely without triggering them). As in the Dungeon, all Temple traps, pressure plates, and wires can be looted by the player for use in player-built mechanisms.

== Notes ==
Even during pre-Hardmode, Lihzahrds and Flying Snakes can spawn outside the Jungle Temple, if the player is standing directly in front of a Lihzahrd Brick Wall (in the Temple's entrance, for example). They pose a very high threat to players with early-game equipment.
Before defeating Plantera, the Lihzahrd Brick Walls will prevent the teleport of following items. The player will not be teleported if the destination is in front of a Lihzahrd Brick Wall.
Teleporters, the Rod of Discord, and the Rod of Harmony will simply not teleport the player.
Pylons will show the status message "Ancient forces prevent you from accessing this location..." when used, and the player will not be teleported.
The player cannot remove any traps other than the Wooden Spikes without a Picksaw or a better pickaxe. It is also impossible to see, cut, or place wires in front of Lihzahrd Brick Walls before defeating the Golem. However, Lihzahrd Pressure Plates are still mineable.
In extremely rare cases, it is possible for the Jungle Temple to generate almost entirely outside of the Underground Jungle, with only a small portion of the edge still touching it. This can happen in worlds even without secret seed world generation modifications to the Jungle Temple. If the player is having trouble looking for the Jungle Temple inside the Jungle, it may be best to check the edges of neighboring biomes.
In a For the Worthy world, the Jungle Temple is 3× bigger than in a normal world. This often means that finding the temple is trivial as it more often than not extends down into the Underworld. Great caution needs to be made, however, as despite being considered in the temple, the Altar in its natural position causes Golem to enter its enraged state due to being in the Underworld. This can also happen in a drunk world.
In a Remix or Zenith world, the Jungle Temple will generate on the surface.

== Tips ==

The Jungle Temple is densely wired. Collecting all wires in the Temple (using the Wire Cutter or The Grand Design) can net a player over 1000 wires.
The Dangersense Potion is useful for avoiding traps, as the buff also highlights Wooden Spikes in addition to traps and pressure plates.
If the player is overwhelmed by Lihzahrds, a way to disable the traps is to shoot at them with some Rocket IV / Mini Nuke II, removing the Pressure Plates and the Wooden Spikes.
The traps can be disarmed by directly removing the wires connecting them to Pressure Plates. The Grand Design can be helpful for doing this.
This can only be done after defeating the Golem.
The Lihzahrd Furnace can be sold for 2 Gold and since every chest inside the temple contains one, selling excess ones is an effective way of making money.
If the player is having trouble locating the temple, consider using the Clentaminator's spray to light up the surrounding environment to highlight the Jungle Temple on the map.


== Trivia ==
The colors of Wires in the Jungle Temple appear to be based on the horizontal position of the pressure plate they originate from.
Life Crystals can generate in the Jungle Temple, although it is extremely rare.
