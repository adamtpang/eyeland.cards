# Goblin Tinkerer

Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:15:28.130Z

---

The Goblin Tinkerer is an NPC vendor who sells several unique items and also provides the reforge function. He will move in once the following criteria have been met:

There is an available vacant house.
Rescuing him as the Bound Goblin, who can be rarely encountered in the Cavern layer (or the Underground layer if playing on the Remix special world seed), after a Goblin Army has been defeated. Interacting with him in this state with ⚷ Open / Activate will free him. If the Bound Goblin is killed, he will still be able to respawn afterwards; if the Goblin Tinkerer NPC is killed, he will move in again after some time as long as there are vacant houses.
The Goblin Tinkerer's reforge option allows the player to randomize the modifier of a weapon or accessory, which makes the resulting item's stats better or worse than they were originally. Reforging costs one-third of the item's current value, which can also be affected by its current modifier. The reforge cost is also affected by the Goblin Tinkerer's happiness, and therefore can be decreased by equipping a Discount Card or Greedy Ring.
The Goblin Tinkerer will defend himself against nearby enemies by throwing Spiky Balls. He deals 15 / 22 / 26 damage at the start of the game, and gains various stat boosts as the player progresses.
The Bound Goblin is one of the few entities that can be detected by the Lifeform Analyzer or its upgrades.

== Shimmered form ==

When submerged in Shimmer or sprayed with the Shimmer Gun, the Goblin Tinkerer will transform into his Shimmered form. This has no impact on gameplay or dialogue and is only a visual change. He can be returned to his classic look by submerging him in Shimmer again or spraying him with the Shimmer Gun again.


== Tips ==
Before the Goblin Tinkerer has been rescued, the Bound Goblin periodically spawns near the player as they travel the Cavern layer, despawning if they get too far away. Therefore, spending hours clearing the map and searching every tunnel is unnecessary.
Consuming a Hunter Potion will cause the Bound Goblin to light up, making him easier to find.
While the Bound Goblin can be found in any open area underground, bound NPCs may be easier for players to find in the Dungeon, as it is an expansive and easily navigated underground space. Bound NPCs can also be found in the Jungle Temple.
As a cost-saving measure, it is beneficial to make the Goblin Tinkerer as happy as possible, so his reforges cost less. This can effectively be done by housing him in the Cavern, Underground, or The Underworld biome along with the Mechanic and the Dye Trader.
For free, most craftable gear can be decrafted with Shimmer and crafted again to reroll its modifier.
In Remix and Zenith worlds, the Bound Goblin is found much higher up as a result of the Cavern layer being relocated to the top of the underground.

== Trivia ==
The Goblin Tinkerer has much shorter ears than other goblins.

The Goblin Tinkerer and the Mechanic have a crush on each other, which is referenced by some of their quotes.
They are also seen flirting during the credits.
It is possible that this is the reason he dislikes the Clothier, considering the Mechanic hates him.
They will frequently flirt when talking to eachother through emotes. The Arms Dealer and Guide will similarly argue through emotes. These are the only four NPCs to have unique interactions when talking to a specific NPC.
The Goblin Tinkerer is depicted alongside the Mechanic in the painting Terrarian Gothic.
The quote, “Yo, I heard you like rockets and running boots, so I put some rockets in your running boots.” is a reference to the Xzibit "Yo Dawg" meme. It is also a reference to how the Tinkerer's Workshop can combine Rocket Boots with running boots (Hermes Boots, Flurry Boots, Dunerider Boots, or Sailfish Boots).
His quote, “I'm working on a Photon Sack. It's an invention to trap all the ghasts lurking around outside!” is likely a reference to the 1984 American supernatural comedy movie Ghostbusters, wherein the protagonists wield Proton Packs to trap ghosts.
One of his names, "Dalek", is a reference to the British science fiction television show Doctor Who. The Daleks are an antagonistic race famed for their advances in technology and desire to exterminate all "inferior" beings.
One of his names, "Gnudar", is possibly a reference to the American animated science fiction sitcom Futurama, as there is an alien named Nudar featured as an antagonist in one of the movies.
Despite his claim “YES, gold is stronger than iron. What are they teaching these humans nowadays?”, iron is more durable than gold in real life, though it remains accurate gameplay-wise.
Unlike other goblins, the Goblin Tinkerer does not drop his weapon (Spiky Balls) if he dies. Ironically, he is also the only goblin to use Spiky Balls as a weapon.
The quote, “Goblins are surprisingly easy to anger. In fact, they could start a war over cloth!” is a reference to the Goblin Battle Standard, one of the ways to summon the Goblin Army event.
 The Bestiary entry for the Goblin Tinkerer: "Exiled from the rest of goblinkind for being an intelligent pacifist, the Tinkerer sells tools for combining accessories into stronger versions."
