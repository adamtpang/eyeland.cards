# King Slime

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/King_Slime
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:16:51.695Z

---

King Slime is a pre-Hardmode boss. Fighting King Slime is not required for game progression, but his defeat may reward the player with useful items. Defeating him for the first time also causes the Nerdy Slime town pet to spawn from the boss.
While King Slime is alive, his exclusive music King Slime will play. When Otherworldly music is enabled, the track Boss 1 (Otherworldly) will play instead.


== Summoning and spawning ==


=== Manual ===
King Slime can be summoned by using the Slime Crown, in any biome at any time.


=== Random ===
King Slime has a 1/300 (0.33%) chance of naturally spawning while the following conditions are met:

The potential spawn point is located in one of the outer lateral sixths of the map (the far left or far right).
The depth at its potential spawn point is above zero.
It is daytime.
King Slime is not currently alive in the world.
The player is not in a Graveyard biome.
There is no water or less than 1 tile of water above its potential spawn point.
There must be standard, green grass at its potential spawn point (off-screen; see NPC spawning)
If the spawn succeeds, King Slime will spawn in a random place around the player and not necessarily on this grass block.


=== Slime Rain ===

If 150 slimes are killed by players, NPCs, traps, lava, and/or summoned minions during the Slime Rain event, King Slime spawns. The following slimes will count toward this total as long as they are killed during the event: Baby Slime, Black Slime, Blue Slime, Green Slime, Jungle Slime, Pinky, Purple Slime, Red Slime, and Yellow Slime. Statue-spawned slimes are eligible as well. Defeating King Slime ends the event shortly after. If all players die, King Slime despawns, and the event continues for some time before ending on its own (between 9 and 15 in-game hours). If King Slime has already been defeated in a world, it only takes 75 slime kills to spawn him. If King Slime is summoned manually with the Slime Crown and then subsequently killed, while the Slime Rain is active, the event ends in the same way as if the player killed the King Slime that naturally spawns at the end of the event. This can be quite helpful for players in later stages of the game who want to get the Slime Rain event over with as quickly as possible.


=== Remix ===

In Remix worlds, killing a slime has a 1/200 (0.5%) chance of spawning King Slime, provided the boss has not yet been defeated, is not already alive, and at least one player has more than 140 maximum health and more than 8 defense. Only the death of certain types of slime can trigger this: Baby Slime, Black Slime, Blue Slime, Green Slime, Jungle Slime, Pinky, Purple Slime, Red Slime, and Yellow Slime. Statue-spawned slimes are eligible as well. Slimes killed without the player dealing damage to them will not spawn King Slime.


== Behavior ==
King Slime attempts to deal contact damage to the player by hopping towards them, and additionally summons slimes to aid him in the fight. When unable to reach the player, he may teleport to their location.
If the player dies or gets more than 187.5 tiles away from King Slime, he will teleport to the bottom-right corner of the world and despawn in 0.17 seconds.


=== Jumping ===
King Slime continually jumps towards the player, cycling through a series of four jumps with varying heights:

Performs a normal jump, reaching a maximum height of 6.42 tiles. Once he lands, there is a cooldown of 1 second before his next jump.
Performs another normal jump with another cooldown of 1 second.
Performs a low jump, reaching a maximum height of 3.56 tiles. Once he lands, there is a cooldown of 1 second before his next jump.
Performs a high jump, reaching a maximum height of 17.2 tiles. Once he lands, there is a cooldown of 1.67 seconds before his next jump.
As King Slime loses health, he will gradually shrink in size (down to 60% of his original size) and the cooldown after his jumps decreases, allowing him to jump more frequently. Reaching certain health thresholds decreases the cooldown, as shown in the table below.


=== Teleporting ===
King Slime can teleport to the player under the following two circumstances:

The line of sight between King Slime and the player is obstructed by solid blocks.
The player's feet are more than 10 tiles below or above the top of King Slime's hitbox.
As soon as either of these conditions is met, a timer of 5 seconds starts ticking down. When it reaches 0, King Slime teleports to the player's location. If it reaches 0 while King Slime is jumping, the teleportation is delayed until he lands on the ground. Afterwards, the timer resets back to 5 seconds. The timer pauses when neither of the two conditions is met, but it is not reset, meaning it continues where it left off once either condition is met again.
In order to teleport to the player, King Slime tries to find a suitable target location with the following steps:

If the total distance between King Slime and the player is greater than 125 tiles, he will teleport directly on top of the player, even if they are midair.
Otherwise, he tries to find a valid block to teleport on that is 7–10 tiles (Chebyshev distance) away from the player's center.
A block is considered valid for teleporting if it is an unactuated solid block (including platforms, but not planter boxes), does not contain lava, and if the tile above it is not occupied by any unactuated, unsloped solid blocks (including platforms and planter boxes). Additionally, King Slime is required to have an unobstructed line of sight to the player after teleporting there.
If the previous step fails, then he tries to find a valid block to teleport on that is 2–6 tiles (Chebyshev distance) away from the player's center.
If all of the previous steps fail, then he will teleport directly on top of the player, even if they are midair.
Once the target location is chosen, King Slime stays still, disappears over the course of 1 second, and then reappears at the target location over the course of 0.5 seconds. His jump attack cycle is reset from the beginning after teleporting.


=== Spawning slimes ===
King Slime can spawn 1–3 Blue Slimes from his body upon taking damage, with each of these Blue Slimes having a 1/4 (25%) chance to be replaced by a Spiked Slime in Expert Mode. At the beginning of the fight, King Slime can spawn slimes upon taking any hit that brings him below 95% health. Afterwards, he needs to lose at least 5% of his maximum health (100 / 140 / 178 health) compared to his health at the last time he spawned slimes before he can spawn them again, with this process repeating until he dies. For example, bringing him to exactly 1,899 / 2,659 / 3,390 (95%) health causes him to spawn slimes for the first time, after which he will not spawn new slimes until brought to below 1,799 / 2,519 / 3,212 (90%) health.
As a result, King Slime has a maximum of 19 opportunities to spawn slimes, meaning 19–57 slimes in total, or 38 slimes on average. This number will be lower if any of King Slime's slime-spawning opportunities is skipped by reducing his health by more than 5% of his maximum health in a single hit.
Finally, King Slime additionally always spawns 4–7 Blue Slimes upon his defeat. These slimes will never be replaced by Spiked Slimes in Expert Mode.


== Aftermath ==
Defeating King Slime for the first time spawns Nerdy Slime.
The Angler will be able to ask for Angelfish and Cloudfish if he could not already.
If Queen Slime has already been defeated, the Princess will begin selling Royal Romance.
The Demolitionist will begin to sell Freeze Bombs during Night if he was not already.
The Traveling Merchant will be able to sell the Gray Zapinator if he could not already.


== Notes ==
Although being a large and resilient enemy, in addition to boss music playing during the fight, a map icon, and a boss health bar appearing, King Slime is often regarded as a "side-boss" or "mini-boss" (though there is no official word on this from the developers). He differs from other bosses in the following ways:
King Slime can be encountered regardless of the current level of game advancement.
He is the only boss alongside Golem that takes damage from lava.
He can spawn at random in a certain area of the world, which is similar to the spawning of regular enemies, rather than bosses.
The Blue Slimes spawned by King Slime can contain bonus drops, meaning they might transform into one of the special slime variants.
King Slime can spawn partially inside of blocks due to his large size, making him unable to move unless through teleporting.
For King Slime's random spawning, the game internally checks if horizontal distance of King Slime's spawning location from the world spawn is greater than 1/3rd of the world width (1400 / 2133 / 2800 tiles in small / medium / large worlds, respectively). In normal world this only allows him to spawn in outer lateral sixths of world, but the spawning area can be different in special or secret world seeds that change the world spawn point, such as Celebration Mk 10 and How did I get here.
In worlds with the For the Worthy seed active, King Slime's body is twice as large in width and height. His horizontal speed during jumps is also increased in this seed, allowing him to cover more distance with jumps (low jumps: 23 mph → 31 mph, normal jumps: 20 mph → 32 mph, high jumps: 18 mph → 31 mph).

== Tips ==

Piercing weapons can not only damage King Slime, but also kill the additional slimes he spawns.
When King Slime is about to teleport, he shrinks and his map icon will get closer towards the ground. The latter can be used to know when he is about to return if he goes offscreen.
The Blue Slimes that are spawned during the fight can drop Hearts and Stars, which respectively restore 20 health and 100 mana.
Due to the high number of Blue Slimes spawned during the fight, summoning King Slime while in specific areas can be a quick, easy way to farm for specific drops. Examples include Biome Keys, Pirate Maps, Halloween and Christmas drops, or the Cascade, Hel-Fire, Amarok, Yelets, and Kraken yoyos.
While Blue Slimes cannot drop Souls of Light or Souls of Night, they can be converted to Shimmer Slimes which can.
King Slime himself does not drop any Gel, but the Blue Slimes he spawns do. This makes fighting him a good way to farm Gel, e.g. for Flamethrower ammo or Asphalt Blocks.
Killing King Slime on Expert Mode and selling all drops gives 10 Gold on average.
When King Slime teleports, he can become stuck inside the blocks around him, preventing his movement for a while.


== Trivia ==
Because the Compass and Depth Meter reveal each tile to be 2 feet in length, King Slime would be about 14′ 4½″  (5.24 m) tall and 15′ 3″ (5.56 m) wide.
The source of the Ninja armor appears to be from the ninja inside King Slime.
According to Redigit, the trapped ninja is the Guide's brother, and he was consumed by King Slime as a result of losing the fight against the boss. This statement was most likely a joke and is not considered official lore.
The trophy dropped by King Slime seems to be a Ninjatō covered in slime, a common weapon that ninjas are usually shown to carry. This may be because King Slime drops the Ninja armor.
The images for the ninja inside King Slime and the crown are stored in separate image files. King Slime's sprite without them looks similar to a massive Blue Slime.
The sprite from 1.1 did not have the crown or the ninja.
Both sprites can be found as Ninja and Extra_39 internally.
Despite sometimes being considered the easiest boss in the game, he does the second highest damage of any pre-Hardmode boss, only beaten by the Wall of Flesh in Classic Mode or Skeletron in Expert Mode.
King Slime is one of the only bosses capable of spawning randomly even after he has already been defeated in the world, the other being Deerclops.
King Slime appears in the video game Core Keeper as part of a crossover.
When teleporting, King Slime drops his current crown and seemingly generates a new one upon completing the teleport.
The Spiked Slime, Pirate's Curse, Jungle Mimic, and Golden Slime are the only non-invincible enemies in the game without a Bestiary entry, and King Slime is the only boss whose summoned enemies do not have one.
 The Bestiary entry for  King Slime: "Slimes normally aren't intelligent, but occasionally they merge together to become a powerful force to swallow all things."
