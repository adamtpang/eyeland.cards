# Guide Voodoo Doll

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Guide_Voodoo_Doll
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:10:45.818Z

---

The Guide Voodoo Doll is a boss-summoning item used to summon the Wall of Flesh. It is a guaranteed drop from Voodoo Demons, a fairly rare enemy in the Underworld.
If the item is dropped and destroyed by lava in the Underworld, the Guide will instantly die and the Wall of Flesh will be summoned. The Guide must be alive for this process to occur. If the item is destroyed by lava elsewhere, the Guide will die as normal but the boss will not be summoned; if the item is destroyed by lava when the Guide is not alive, the item will be wasted without summoning the boss.
On the  Desktop version,  Console version, and  Mobile version, if more than one Guide Voodoo Doll is in a stack upon being destroyed by lava, other NPCs will be killed alongside the Guide depending on the amount within the stack. However, the Wall of Flesh will only be summoned once.
When the Guide Voodoo Doll is equipped as an accessory, it becomes possible for the player and other NPCs to kill the Guide with direct attacks from weapons (which normally would not damage him). 
The Guide Voodoo Doll cannot be reforged, and therefore cannot have modifiers.


== Notes ==

Only one Wall of Flesh can be summoned at once. Even if a new Guide moves in while the Wall of Flesh is still alive, throwing another Guide Voodoo Doll into lava will not summon the boss a second time.

== Trivia ==
In popular culture, a voodoo doll is a figurine resembling a person, which can be harmed in order to magically inflict harm on the person being mimicked.
The tooltip is likely a reference to a quote from the character GLaDOS from the 2011 puzzle-platforming game Portal 2, "You are a horrible person. [...] We weren't even testing for that."
The last sentence of the quote is also referenced as the name of the secret world seed We don't even test for that.
Before the Wall of Flesh is defeated, several NPCs sometimes make references to the doll's function:
Arms Dealer: “I heard there is a doll that looks very similar to <name of Guide> somewhere in the underworld.  I'd like to put a few rounds in it.”
Nurse: “I need to have a serious talk with <name of Guide>. How many times a week can you come in with severe lava burns?”
Guide: “When you are ready to challenge the keeper of the underworld, you will have to make a living sacrifice. Everything you need for it can be found in the underworld.”
