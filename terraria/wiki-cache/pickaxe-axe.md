# Pickaxe Axe

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Pickaxe_Axe
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:11:44.994Z

---

The Pickaxe Axe is a Hardmode, post-mechanical bosses pickaxe and axe. It is the alternative to the Drax, having a lower base mining speed but longer range and the ability to benefit from mining speed bonuses. It can mine blocks up to Chlorophyte Ore.
Its best modifier is Legendary. However, due to rounding, Legendary, Light, Deadly, Agile, Quick, and Nasty provide equal boosts to mining speed for this tool.

== Trivia ==
Unlike other Hallow-themed items, it does not emit light when used.
The tooltip references a "hamdrill", which may be a callback to the Drax and its behavior before the 1.2 update, being named Hamdrax while having the functionality of a hammer, drill, and chainsaw.
