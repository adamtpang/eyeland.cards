# Old Man

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Man
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:15:29.761Z

---

The Old Man is an NPC whose primary function is to summon Skeletron, and can be found at the Dungeon's entrance. When spoken to at night, the Old Man will give the player the option to “Curse”, which summons Skeletron. When spoken to during the day, the Old Man will tell the player to return at night. He will also judge players with 300 maximum health (15 hearts) or less, or 10 defense or less as unworthy, but still allows them to summon Skeletron.
The Dungeon can be entered without defeating Skeletron, but traveling deeper than ground level within it (below zero depth) will spawn Dungeon Guardians that will most likely kill the player on contact (see Guide:Dungeon Guardian strategies).
When Skeletron is defeated, the Old Man is freed from his curse and will arrive as the Clothier (as long as a vacant house is available), in addition to allowing access to the Dungeon.


== Shimmered form ==

When submerged in Shimmer or sprayed with the Shimmer Gun, the Old Man will transform into his Shimmered form. This has no impact on gameplay or dialogue and is only a visual change. He can be returned to his classic look by submerging him in Shimmer again or spraying him with the Shimmer Gun again.


== Notes ==
The Old Man can be hurt with lava, Dart Traps, which cause normal damage and debuff effects, and explosives. He will also take damage from a rolling Boulder and Suffocation like all other NPCs. However, enemies cannot hurt him, and he does not defend himself against them (unlike other NPCs), although he will still run away from them.
If the Old Man dies or the player fails to defeat Skeletron, the Old Man will respawn at the Dungeon entrance the following day (at 6:30 AM).
When respawning in this way, the Old Man can spawn on-screen.

== Trivia ==
Other NPCs will "interact" and emote with the Old Man and engage in conversations.
He is the only NPC that will not defend himself and is not damaged by enemies. He also cannot be drowned.
He is one of only two friendly NPCs that does not have a map icon, the other being the Skeleton Merchant.
The Old Man is depicted in the painting The Cursed Man.
Upon summoning Skeletron, the Old Man dies, which makes it seem as if he turns into Skeletron, further supported by his Bestiary entry.
He can, however, respawn the following day while Skeletron is still alive but dies if Skeletron is beaten.
The Old Man's Shimmered form is reminiscent of the Shimmered form of the Clothier.
 The Bestiary entry for the Old Man: "This hapless Old Man carries the burden of a heavy curse. It's said at night he transforms into a horrific demon which guards the Dungeon."
