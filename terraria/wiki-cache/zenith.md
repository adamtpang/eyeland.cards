# Zenith

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Zenith
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:11:57.230Z

---

The Zenith is a Hardmode, post-Moon Lord melee weapon. When used, it rapidly fires fast-moving sword sprites towards the cursor in an arc returning to the player. The sword sprites can pass through blocks, pierce enemies infinitely, home in on nearby enemies, and create visual trails while traveling. The sword sprites have varying appearances, which function identically and have the same hitbox size. The Zenith has a base 14% critical strike chance as opposed to the usual 4%.
The Zenith will transform into the True Copper Shortsword when thrown into Shimmer.
Its best modifier is Legendary.

== Notes ==
With the exception of the first sword fired, the sword sprites home in on enemies within a 20-tile radius of the cursor, regardless of the cursor's location.
The first sword generated on each swing will always be a Zenith projectile.
The Zenith can only reach locations within a 1920-pixel×1200-pixel (120-tile×75-tile) rectangular area centered on the player's center. However, if part of this area is out of visible world borders, the whole area will be moved horizontally and/or vertically to ensure all area is within world border.
Increasing melee speed affects the number of swords that appear per use, but not the number of swords thrown per second or the swords' velocity. At default melee speed, 3 swords will appear per use. Higher melee speed will cause fewer swords besides the Zenith to appear. With high enough melee speed, it is possible for only the Zenith itself to appear. However, the weapon will lose the ability to home in on enemies, since the Zenith itself never homes
Weapon modifiers that change attack speed will actually affect Zenith's attack rate and DPS, as opposed to melee speed bonuses.
Each sword sprite can hit 8 times per second, and may hit multiple times on a single enemy, depending on size and positioning.
Swords also deal damage behind the player while returning.
The Zenith is fully bright in the dark when dropped as an item, placed inside a Weapon Rack, Item Frame, or Item Flask. The sword projectiles leave behind light-emitting fully bright particles.

== Tips ==
The Zenith is widely considered the strongest weapon in Terraria (along with its reskinned variant).
If the player had made a Journey Mode character or has lost their Copper Shortsword and has no Copper Ore in the world, Copper can be obtained by using a Chlorophyte Extractinator with Tin Ore or Tin Bars, fishing, using an Extractinator, killing slimes for bonus drops, dipping Tin Ore into Shimmer, or bringing some ore from a different world which has Copper.


== Trivia ==

In regular combat, most players will see the Zenith dealing around 20,000 to 30,000 DPS. However, it has the potential to reach much higher DPS numbers:
Against most single targets, it can reach more than 70,000 DPS with the right armor, accessories, modifiers, buffs, and correct positioning.
Against worm enemies like The Destroyer, it can surpass 1,000,000 DPS.
In an optimized environment, the Zenith can do more than 7,000,000 DPS.
The Zenith can defeat the Moon Lord on Master Mode (277,311 combined HP) in about 11 seconds.
The word zenith is defined as "the time/point at which something is at its most strength or successful". This ties in with the fact that the Zenith is often considered the final and best weapon in the game.
The Zenith represents the player's journey, as implied from the achievement description and crafting materials.
 Before the release of the 1.4.0.1 update, the sword had various placeholders and scrapped names, including Final Fractal and Summit. The precursor design of the Zenith exists as an unobtainable weapon, the First Fractal, which resembles an upgraded Terra Blade.
The Zenith has the most complex crafting tree in Terraria, consisting of 22 different base items; surpassing the Shellphone (16 items), Cell Phone (14 items), and Ankh Shield (12 items).
Using Teleporters and portals while using the Zenith causes each sword to move in sharp angles and strange directions.

The Zenith projectiles will always include both the Light's Bane and Blood Butcherer, no matter which one was used for crafting it.
The Zenith's projectiles include the Terragrim, despite not being in its crafting tree.
The Bee Keeper has been in the Zenith's animation since its release in 1.4.0.1, but was only added to the crafting recipe in the 1.4.0.3 hotfix.
Despite being nearly identical to its copper counterpart, the Tin Shortsword cannot be used to craft the Zenith. This is presumably due to the Copper Shortsword's role as the very first sword every player will encounter (with the exception of Journey Mode characters).
