# Eye of Cthulhu

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Eye_of_Cthulhu
Fetched: 2026-08-20T07:58:51.718Z

---

The Eye of Cthulhu is a pre-Hardmode boss. It is typically one of the first bosses a player may encounter, as it spawns automatically when a relatively early level of game advancement is achieved. It can also be summoned manually with the Suspicious Looking Eye at night (see Summoning and spawning below).
While the Eye of Cthulhu is alive, its exclusive music Boss 1 will play. When Otherworldly music is enabled, the track Boss 1 (Otherworldly) will play instead.


== Summoning and spawning ==


=== Manual ===
The Eye of Cthulhu can be summoned manually using a Suspicious Looking Eye at night (from 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM). Only one Eye of Cthulhu can be summoned manually at a time.


=== Random ===
The Eye of Cthulhu can also spawn naturally at the start of the night. Its arrival when spawning naturally is announced by the status message “You feel an evil presence watching you...”.
In order for the boss to spawn naturally, the following conditions must be fulfilled at dusk (7:30 PM):

The Eye of Cthulhu has not yet been defeated in the current world or no placed Demon/Crimson Altars exist in the current world.
At least one player in the world has at least 200 maximum health and at least 11 defense.
At least four town NPCs are present in the world. While the Old Man, Traveling Merchant, and town pets count towards this total, the Skeleton Merchant does not.
No boss is already alive.
None of the following events are active: Lantern Night, Old One's Army, Pumpkin Moon, or Frost Moon.
Time is not being accelerated by the Enchanted Sundial or Enchanted Moondial.
If the conditions are met, there is a 1/3 (33.33%) chance for the Eye of Cthulhu to spawn. Provided the roll succeeds, the status message appears and a hidden countdown of 1 minute 21 seconds begins. The Eye of Cthulhu spawns at the end of the countdown, at 8:51 PM. Another check is performed at that time, with all of the following conditions needing to be fulfilled in order for the boss to spawn:

At least one player is in the surface or Space layer.
Consequently, the natural spawning of the Eye of Cthulhu can easily be prevented by remaining in a layer lower than the surface for the night. Returning to the surface before dawn will cause it to spawn as normal.
This restriction is lifted in worlds where the world spawn point is below the surface, such as worlds with the special world seed Remix active or the secret world seed Mole people. The Eye of Cthulhu can spawn naturally in those worlds regardless of the player's position, whether they are above or below the surface.
No boss is already alive.
The Lunar Events are not active and the countdown for the spawning of the Moon Lord has not begun, either due to manual summoning or defeating the fourth Celestial Pillar.
Time is not being accelerated by the Enchanted Sundial or Enchanted Moondial.
In singleplayer, exiting the world during the countdown also prevents the Eye of Cthulhu from spawning naturally.


== Behavior ==
The Eye of Cthulhu has two phases and is able to fly through blocks in both of them. During the first, it attempts to float directly above the player while spawning 3–4 Servants of Cthulhu, which drop Hearts and Stars on death. A servant is spawned with the typical hit sound. Afterward, it charges at the player three times in succession, before repeating the cycle.
When the Eye of Cthulhu falls below 50% / 65% health (1,400 / 2,366 / 3,016), it spins in place and transforms into its second form (shedding its iris into a mouth), which rams the player more aggressively three times, then hangs in the air for a period. The Eye of Cthulhu no longer spawns Servants of Cthulhu and has no defense, but now has increased contact damage. It also now lets out a roar every time it charges.
If the Eye of Cthulhu is not defeated by dawn (4:30 AM), if all players die, or if it goes too far off-screen, it will despawn.


=== Expert Mode ===
In Expert Mode, the Eye of Cthulhu has the following extra properties:

The Eye of Cthulhu spawns more Servants of Cthulhu than normal, as well as 4–5 extra Servants of Cthulhu upon transforming.
In the second phase, the Eye of Cthulhu will gain an additional powerful rapid chain dash attack, during which it lets out a higher-pitched roar every time it dashes. At first, the Eye of Cthulhu's chain dashes are performed after its regular ones but they will eventually replace the standard dashes and become faster and trigger more often as the Eye of Cthulhu's life decreases, dashing nearly continuously when close to death.
The Eye of Cthulhu will have negative defense when it reaches very low health (which increases damage dealt to it):
−15 defense when below 12% health (436 / 556), which means it takes 7 more damage per hit (15 more damage from critical hits).
−30 defense when below 4% health (145 / 185), which means it takes 15 more damage per hit (30 more damage from critical hits).


== Aftermath ==
The Angler will be able to ask for Angelfish and Cloudfish if he could not already.
The Dryad will be able to move in if she could not already.
The Traveling Merchant will begin selling Code 1, and will begin selling the Gray Zapinator if he was not already.


== Notes ==
Only one Eye of Cthulhu can be summoned at a time. Nothing will happen if the Suspicious Looking Eye is used while the Eye of Cthulhu is currently alive or the time is unsuitable.
However, if one uses the item after the natural spawn message appears but before that eye spawns, a second eye will still spawn.
Additionally, if a player in Journey Mode rapidly switches between dawn and dusk, preventing the eye from despawning, more naturally spawned eyes can appear.
The conditions of whether an altar can be dropped is checked upon entering the world, or occasionally during gameplay. If no altars were found (i.e. no tiles with ID 26 exist in world), the Eye of Cthulhu will drop an altar upon its defeat and reset the drop condition (i.e. it cannot drop altars again until another check is performed and passed).
In a Drunk world, the type of altar dropped still corresponds to the world evil chosen upon generation.


== Achievements ==


== Tips ==

The Eye of Cthulhu drops either Demonite or Crimtane Ore. While the corresponding set of armor and a number of tools additionally require Shadow Scales or Tissue Samples respectively—which the Eye does not drop—the sword, bow, axe, yoyo, whip, and fishing pole can be crafted with only the bars.
Due to the ease of crafting its summoning item and relatively low difficulty, farming the Eye of Cthulhu is one of the more effective ways of making money prior to Hardmode. It drops 3 Gold / 7 Gold 50 Silver up front, plus ores which can be sold as bars for twice (Demonite) or triple (Crimtane) that amount.
The Eye of Cthulhu will only spawn randomly if a player has more than 10 defense. Taking defense-boosting equipment off before dusk can prevent it from spawning randomly.
The boss looks at the player it will follow, which can be helpful information in multiplayer.
It is most vulnerable during its transformation, because it neither moves nor attacks. However, there is only a brief window of opportunity for this.
Because there are no location-based requirements for summoning, defeating multiple Eyes of Cthulhu in an area can be an effective setup for harder boss fights as the dropped Hearts can be picked up mid-fight as needed, though killing an Eye of Cthulhu for that purpose must be balanced with the time it takes away from killing a night-only boss.
Since Crimtane Ore and Bars sell for a 30% higher price than their Demonite counterparts, farming the Eye of Cthulhu in a Crimson world is more profitable.
In singleplayer, the player can repeatedly pause the game using Autopause in order to properly aim at the Eye of Cthulhu during its dash attacks.


== Trivia ==
The Eye of Cthulhu, among several other bosses, is based on Cthulhu, a creature first imagined by American author H. P. Lovecraft in his short story The Call of Cthulhu.
The existence of the True Eye of Cthulhu, an entity spawned by the Moon Lord (who bears resemblance to Cthulhu), may imply that the Eye of Cthulhu is not Cthulhu's actual true eye.
The lore pages and the Eye of Cthulhu's Bestiary entry both contradict this assumption.
Because the Compass and Depth Meter reveal each tile to be 2 feet in length, the Eye of Cthulhu would be about 13′ 9″ (5.01 m) tall and wide in its first form. The teeth in its second form would be about 2′ 7½″ (0.96 m) long.
The Merchant NPC will mention sensing an evil presence watching him once the requirements to battle the Eye of Cthulhu are met.
Although irrelevant to game progression, the Eye of Cthulhu will keep spawning naturally until defeated at least once.
It is possible to defeat the Eye of Cthulhu without it ever transforming into its second form, e.g. with late-game weapons. However, its gore will be unaffected once killed.
The Eye of Cthulhu Trophy is a tooth of the Eye of Cthulhu, which is only physically seen in its second form.
The Eye of Cthulhu seems to have central heterochromia iridum, an eye condition where there are two colors in the same iris.
The Eye of Cthulhu, alongside the Eater of Worlds and Skeletron were the first bosses added into Terraria, debuting with the game's release in update 1.0.
The Eye of Cthulhu is sometimes abbreviated as "EoC".
(Desktop version) If the player is using an RGB-enabled keyboard, a miniature Eye of Cthulhu can be seen dashing from the left to the right during the battle.
The Eye of Cthulhu can sometimes be seen flying in the background as ambience at night.
Two clouds shaped like the Eye of Cthulhu's first and second forms can also appear in the background.
The Eye of Cthulhu appears as a boss in three games via crossover events: Dungeon Defenders 2, Don't Starve Together, and Palworld. In Don't Starve Together, it is called "Eye of Terror" instead.
The Eye of Cthulhu appears in the daytime instead of at night in some artwork for Terraria. This is most prominent in the PS3 cover and the Xbox 360 cover.
 – According to the Terraria lore pages, the Eye of Cthulhu is one of the organs ripped out from Cthulhu when he was defeated.
 The Bestiary entry for the Eye of Cthulhu: "A piece of Cthulhu ripped from his body centuries ago in a bloody war. It wanders the night seeking its host body... and revenge!"
