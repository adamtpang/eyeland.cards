# Suspicious Looking Eye

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Suspicious_Looking_Eye
Fetched: 2026-08-20T07:59:59.184Z

---

The Suspicious Looking Eye is a boss-summoning item that summons the Eye of Cthulhu when used. It can be used in any biome, but only at night.
In addition to being crafted, Suspicious Looking Eyes also have a 1/5 (20%) chance of being found in Ivy Chests and Gold Chests found in the Cavern layer, including locked ones found inside the Dungeon.

== Notes ==
It is possible to legitimately fight two Eye of Cthulhus at the same time: if the message “You feel an evil presence watching you...” appears, an Eye can be summoned, but this will not cancel out the Eye that was already set to appear, resulting in a second Eye joining the fight shortly after the first Eye spawns.


== Trivia ==
The Suspicious Looking Eye is the upside-down icon of a Servant of Cthulhu.
It is one of the few suspicious-looking boss-summoning items in Terraria, the others being the Suspicious Looking Skull(Old-gen console and 3DS versions) and the Suspicious Looking Egg(3DS version).
