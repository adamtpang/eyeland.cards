# Gold Crown

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold_Crown
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:18:05.613Z

---

The Gold Crown is a craftable vanity item that can be worn in the Social Helmet inventory slot. It is also one of the crafting materials for the Slime Crown, which is used to spawn King Slime. 
Its alternate ore counterpart is the Platinum Crown.
