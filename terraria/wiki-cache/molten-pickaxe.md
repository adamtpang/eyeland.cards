# Molten Pickaxe

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Molten_Pickaxe
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:10:48.777Z

---

The Molten Pickaxe is a pre-Hardmode pickaxe. It can mine blocks up to Dungeon Bricks, Cobalt Ore, and Palladium Ore. The Molten Pickaxe occasionally inflicts the On Fire! debuff on hit enemies.
Its best modifier is Light for harvesting purposes and Legendary for combat purposes.

== Notes ==
The Molten Pickaxe is fully bright in the dark when used, dropped, or placed inside a Weapon Rack, an Item Frame, or an Item Flask. It also releases light-emitting particles when swung.

== Tips ==
Although the Molten Pickaxe can destroy most blocks it can mine in one hit, its slow mining speed means that lower tiered pickaxes can mine the weakest block types faster (namely the Bone Pickaxe). However, it remains the fastest pre-Hardmode pickaxe for mining Stone Blocks and above.


== Trivia ==
The achievement associated with the Molten Pickaxe depicts its pre-1.4.0.3 sprite.
The Molten Pickaxe is one of two pickaxes that have unique mining capabilities (i.e. the pickaxe power necessary to mine a certain set of blocks), the other being the Tungsten Pickaxe.
