# Skeletron Prime

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Skeletron_Prime
Fetched: 2026-08-20T07:59:36.761Z

---

Skeletron Prime is a Hardmode mechanical boss which is essentially a more difficult version of Skeletron. It consists of a head and four limbs, but, similarly to Skeletron, only the head needs to be defeated to defeat the boss.
While Skeletron Prime is alive, its exclusive music Skeletron Prime will play. When Otherworldly music is enabled, the track Boss 2 (Otherworldly) will play instead.


== Summoning and spawning ==


=== Manual ===
Skeletron Prime can be summoned manually using a Mechanical Skull at night (from 7:30 PM to 4:30 AM).


=== Random ===
Skeletron Prime can also spawn naturally at the start of the night, as long as it has not yet been defeated at least once in the current world. Its arrival when spawning naturally is announced by the status message "The air is getting colder around you...".
In order for the boss to spawn naturally, the following conditions must be fulfilled at dusk (7:30 PM):

The world is in Hardmode and at least one Demon or Crimson Altar has been destroyed.
In worlds where the Skyblock special world seed is active and the tile threshold is not exceeded, Skeletron Prime can spawn even if no altars have been destroyed.
No boss is already alive, and the Eye of Cthulhu is not already spawning naturally.
None of the following events are active: Lantern Night, Old One's Army, Pumpkin Moon, or Frost Moon.
Time is not being accelerated by the Enchanted Sundial or Enchanted Moondial.
If the conditions are met, there is a 1/10 (10%) chance for a random mechanical boss to spawn that has not yet been defeated in the world. This gives Skeletron Prime initially a spawn chance of 1/30 (3.33%) per night. If another mechanical boss is defeated first, the chance for Skeletron Prime to spawn increases to 1/20 (5%), and should both of the other mechanical bosses be defeated first, then Skeletron Prime has the full remaining 1/10 (10%) spawn chance per night.
Once Skeletron Prime has been selected to spawn, the status message appears and a hidden countdown of 1 minute 21 seconds begins. Skeletron Prime spawns at the end of the countdown, at 8:51 PM. Another check is performed at that time, with all of the following conditions needing to be fulfilled in order for the boss to spawn:

At least one player is in the surface or Space layer.
Consequently, the natural spawning of Skeletron Prime can easily be prevented by being in a layer lower than the surface at 8:51 PM. Returning to the surface afterward will not cause Skeletron Prime to spawn either (unlike with the Eye of Cthulhu).
This restriction is lifted in worlds where the world spawn point is below the surface, such as worlds with the special world seed Remix active or the secret world seed Mole people. Skeletron Prime can spawn naturally in those worlds regardless of the player's position, whether they are above or below the surface.
No boss is already alive.
The Lunar Events are not active and the countdown for the spawning of the Moon Lord has not begun, either due to manual summoning or defeating the fourth Celestial Pillar.
Time is not being accelerated by the Enchanted Sundial or Enchanted Moondial.
In singleplayer, exiting the world during the countdown also prevents Skeletron Prime from spawning naturally.


=== For the Worthy and Remix ===
Skeletron Prime cannot be summoned in worlds where both the For the Worthy and Remix special world seeds are active, since the boss is part of Mechdusa instead.


== Arms ==


== Behavior ==
Skeletron Prime's head constantly endeavors to remain between 31.5 and 12.5 tiles above the player and within 6.25 tiles of either side of the player. It can accelerate infinitely in order to move back into this area. The head does not actively attempt to make contact with the player.
Skeletron Prime's four limbs all have unique attacks:

Prime Cannon fires bombs every 2.33 seconds that do not destroy tiles, usually up into the air rather than directly at the player. It usually stays above the player.
Prime Saw usually remains under the player, moving up and down.
Prime Vice will swipe around wildly, making occasional lunges at the player. It usually remains diagonal to the player.
Prime Laser shoots red Death Lasers every 3.33 seconds, similar to those of Retinazer and The Destroyer, and is always pointed at a player. It usually stays horizontal to the player.

Every 10 seconds, Skeletron Prime will enter a spinning phase for 6.67 seconds, in which its attacks become more aggressive:

Skeletron Prime's head will grow spikes and start spinning towards the player at 10 mph, exactly like how its pre-Hardmode counterpart Skeletron does. Damage and defense are doubled to 94 / 160 / 240 damage and 48 defense. In Expert Mode, the base spinning speed is 31 mph and is increased the further the player is away, up to a maximum of 76 mph at a distance of 37.5 tiles.
Prime Cannon will aim at the player directly and decrease its cooldown between firing to 0.67 seconds.
Prime Saw will begin homing directly towards the player's location.
Prime Vice becomes more aggressive and frequently lunges towards the player.
Prime Laser will fire lasers every 1.33 seconds.
Skeletron Prime lets out a roar every time it enters the spinning phase.
If Skeletron Prime is not killed before dawn (4:30 AM), it will enrage and permanently behave like a faster Dungeon Guardian, increasing its velocity and usually killing the player instantly upon contact: its damage and defense is increased to 9,999. It will remain in this state until defeated, even if it becomes night again. Killing Skeletron Prime in this form still counts as defeating it and it will drop its items normally.
If the player is more than 375 tiles away, Skeletron Prime will start moving downwards and will quickly despawn.


== Notes ==
The Bombs fired by the Prime Cannon explode upon contact with platforms as well as Planter Boxes.


== Achievements ==


== Tips ==

Unhindered movement is crucial when Skeletron Prime's head starts spinning, as it deals double the regular damage. Wings greatly aid in moving vertically, and flattened terrain or a sky bridge are convenient for horizontal movement.
Although it is a little more time-consuming, focusing on the arms first is very rewarding. Once Skeletron Prime's head is no longer shielded by anything, the fight becomes a lot easier. Prioritize damage on the arm which is causing the most damage or disruption, so it can be destroyed and removed from the fight as quick as possible.
A good idea is to focus on the Prime Laser first, as it is harder to dodge than the other limbs.
Due to Skeletron Prime's overall high defense, inflicting it with Ichor is effective, as it is not immune to it. Note that most Ichor weapons lack in damage compared to other weapons, so they should only be used periodically for inflicting the debuff.


== Trivia ==

Skeletron Prime will open and shut its mouth for the entire duration of the battle, except for when its head is spinning. This is a purely cosmetic effect.
Skeletron Prime's joints that connect its arms and head continuously emit fire and smoke particles in a similar manner to that of Skeletron's joints.
It is the only mechanical boss whose name contains the pre-Hardmode boss's name. Furthermore, it used to be the only mechanical boss that shared boss music with its pre-Hardmode variant. Additionally, its name does not begin with the word The, unlike the other two mechanical bosses.
Unlike Skeletron, Skeletron Prime's defense doubles when its head is spinning.
Although Skeletron Prime's head is immune to Penetrated, its limbs are not.
Normally, Skeletron Prime's head has 2000 less health than Plantera, but in Expert Mode and Master Mode, they both have the same amount.
When Skeletron Prime's head is spinning, the map icon will spin too.
Skeletron Prime is the only boss with more than two arms.
The sprite file for Skeletron Prime's arms is named Arm_Bone_2.
 – According to the Terraria lore pages, Skeletron Prime was one of the three mechanical recreations of Cthulhu's missing organs. It is a recreation of part of Cthulhu's skeleton.
 The Bestiary entry for  Skeletron Prime: "Mechanically reconstructed for reviving Cthulhu, this Skeletron has more arms than ever before, and a variety of fierce weapons."
