# Witch Doctor

Source: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Witch_Doctor
Fetched: 2026-08-20T08:15:31.300Z

---

The Witch Doctor is an NPC vendor who will move in once the following criteria have been met:

There is an available vacant house.
The Queen Bee has been defeated.
The Witch Doctor will defend himself against nearby enemies by shooting Poison Darts from a Blowgun that damage and poison enemies for 30–60 seconds. He deals 20 / 30 / 35 damage at the start of the game, and gains various stat boosts as the player progresses.

== Shimmered form ==

When submerged in Shimmer or sprayed with the Shimmer Gun, the Witch Doctor will transform into his Shimmered form. This has no impact on gameplay or dialogue and is only a visual change. He can be returned to his classic look by submerging him in Shimmer again or spraying him with the Shimmer Gun again.


== Notes ==
The biome requirements for the Witch Doctor's shop selections are determined by the location of the player speaking to him, not the location of the Witch Doctor himself. Thus, it technically does not matter what biome the Witch Doctor is in, as long as the player interacting with him is in the correct biome (e.g. Jungle).


== Tips ==
Players can teleport the Witch Doctor to the Jungle using the King Statue.

== Trivia ==
A witch doctor is a term for shamans and "wise men" of various traditions, including the European "witch doctor", the African “sangoma”, and the Native American “medicine man”. It originally referred to one who protects against witchcraft, but contemporary use refers to any folk healer.
Despite being a folk healer, the Witch Doctor in Terraria cannot heal the player.
The quote “Which doctor am I? The Witch Doctor am I.” plays on the homophone "which/witch". Both sentences sound almost identical but have very different meanings.
The quote “Rise, zombie! Zombie, arise!” is a reference to the memetically famous "Chicken, Arise" sequence from the adult animated television series Aqua Teen Hunger Force, in which the protagonists recruit the aid of a somewhat dubious witch doctor who uses a similar chant in a protracted effort to resurrect a chicken.
Many of his quotes contain two sentences that use the same words to play on each other.
The Witch Doctor is a Lihzahrd, as can be seen by his green skin and tail. This was confirmed by Leinfors on the Terraria forums and is also mentioned in his Bestiary entry.
This is also evident by his party quote "I wanted to see how your kind celebrate", and his happiness quote when near the Princess: "Kind to my people, <name of Princess> is. Much appreciated."
Despite the happiness quote when near the Princess indicates that the Witch Doctor has good relationship with the Lihzahrds, Lihzahrds will still attack the Witch Doctor (and vice-versa), unlike the Skeleton Merchant where Skeletons will not attack it (and vice-versa).
The staff that the Witch Doctor holds may look like a Pygmy Staff, but it is actually a “dreamcatcher”, a wooden circle atop a stick, with strings resembling a spiders web and feathers that hang from them. They originate from Native American tribes as protective charms.
 The Bestiary entry for the Witch Doctor: "It's unusual for a Lihzahrd to be outside the Temple. Even more unusual are the voodoo things he sells. He prefers his jungle digs."
