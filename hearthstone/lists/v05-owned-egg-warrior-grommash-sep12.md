# Egg Warrior: paired Ooze and Grommash

2026-09-12. Proposed test, not performance-validated.

From v04: -2 Slam, +1 Dissolving Ooze, +1 Grommash Hellscream. All cards owned in today's collection snapshot; 0 dust. Every non-Legendary remains paired.

Grommash converts Ooze Bones into immediate Charge damage and works with the existing self-damage package. Hogger also duplicates him. Cut Slam as the overlapping damage/draw slot while preserving Eternal Toil, Acolyte and For Glory refill. Cost: less cheap cycling and a heavier opening hand; two 1-mana cards become a 3-mana and an 8-mana card (+9 total printed mana, +0.30 average). Hogger adds another expensive copy at game start.

Ooze consuming a 20-Attack Khelos on an earlier turn makes +20-Attack Bones. On a later turn, Grommash (8) plus Bones (2) gives 24 Charge attack before any damage bonus; enemy Taunts still matter.
