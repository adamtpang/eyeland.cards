# Death wizard path: Ship of Fools and Deer Knight

Written 2026-09-14. Starting point: Death wizard, level 94, questing in
Khrysalis. Membership bought, Deer Knight recipe bought, solo deck set.

**Goal:** permanently learn Ship of Fools and Deer Knight, without wasting
time or cash.

**Honest framing first:** neither spell is required. Death already solos well
with Scarecrow. Ship of Fools is a real upgrade (a cheap hit-all that heals
you). Deer Knight is also a hit-all: 3 pips plus 1 Death pip, 85% accuracy,
300 Death damage plus 270 over 3 rounds to all enemies (Wizard101 Central,
checked 2026-09-14). So quest forward and pick the spells up along the way;
do not stop progressing to farm them.

**Free routes only** (Adam's choice, 2026-09-14). No Crown packs.

**Confirmed 2026-09-14 on Wizard101 Central:** Drowned Dan (Tier 3) drops
Ship of Fools Spellements, alongside Celestial Intervention, Nautilus
Unleashed, and Spiritual Tribunal. Ship of Fools is farmable for free. His
drop list also includes Gold Skeleton Keys.

## Optimal path (do in this order)

Ordered so every trip does as much as possible. Reference details for each
step are in the sections further down.

**Why this order:** Deer Knight needs no gold keys (every ingredient except
Amber is already in hand), so it comes first. The crafting chain runs through
low-level worlds that go fast on a mount at level 94, and several stops
overlap with other tasks. Ship of Fools needs gold keys, which come fastest at
level 100, so it comes last.

## The bottleneck: gold skeleton keys

Both spells unlock at **35 Spellements** each, and both come from gold key
bosses:

| Spell | Boss | Where | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ship of Fools | Drowned Dan | Celestia, Science Center, Drowned Dan's Chamber | 1 gold key |
| Deer Knight | Lambent Fire | Avalon, Crystal Caves, near the entrance | 1 gold key |

Each boss fight drops about **14 to 16 Spellements**, but they are spread
across every spell in that boss's pack. How many land on your spell per run is
**unconfirmed**, so expect more than three runs per spell. Count your first
few runs and update this file.

Old guides send you to the Loremaster for Deer Knight. **That is outdated.**
Lambent Fire is the current Deer Knight source.

### Solo deck build (level 94, Khrysalis)

Added 2026-09-16 from general Wizard101 knowledge, not a source pulled that
day. Verify spell levels in the spellbook; the principles hold.

Rule 1: small deck, about 20 to 28 cards. Every filler card is a turn spent
drawing the wrong thing. Only cards you would cast in an average fight.

Rule 2: one kill pattern, copied many times. Death solo is blade, Feint,
hit-all.

| Slot | Cards | Copies |
|---|---|---:|
| Blades | Death Blade | 3 to 4 |
| Blades | Spirit Blade (if trained) | 2 |
| Traps | Feint (best card in the deck) | 3 to 4 |
| Traps | Death Trap | 2 |
| Hit-all | Scarecrow now, Deer Knight once crafted | 3 to 4 |
| Boss hit | Skeletal Dragon or Dr. Von's Monster | 2 |
| Defense | Tower Shield | 2 |
| Defense | Death Dispel, only vs Death bosses | 1 to 2 |
| Utility | Reshuffle | 1 |
| Utility | Pierce or Shatter, for shield-heavy Khrysalis bosses | 2 |
| Heal | Sacrifice, or Satyr if Life is trained, only if dying | 1 to 2 |

- Enchant every hit with the highest Sun damage enchant trained (Colossal at
  this level, Epic if Colossal is not trained).
- Enchant during the fight, not in the deck screen, so an enchant is never
  stuck on a card that does not get cast.
- Side deck (treasure cards): Feint, Death Blade, Colossal, a Dragonblade or
  two from the Bazaar. Boss-only cards live here, not in the main deck.
- Khrysalis mobs have high health: plan two-round setups (blade plus Feint,
  then hit) instead of one-round kills.
- Once Deer Knight is crafted it replaces Scarecrow: its damage over time
  ticks past the Fire and Storm shields Khrysalis mobs carry.

### Getting 10 Amber (free)

1. **Farm Ra:** Krokotopia, The Oasis, Ra's Reading Room. Rank 4, 3,000

## Step 4: Farm Darkmoor for gold keys (level 100)

Three dungeons, about **2 hours** per full clear with a team that knows the
cheats. Go with a team (Team Up kiosk or a Discord farming group). Membership
covers access; without it, 3,495 Crowns permanent or 495 for 6 hours.

| Dungeon | Boss | Cheat | Counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Castle Darkmoor | Howling Chaney | Gnomes anyone who heals; must be hit every round | Feints and blades; Death damage-over-time counts as a hit |
| Castle Darkmoor | Sir Blackwater | Strips single blades and universal traps | Feint the same round you attack; Aegis your blades |
| Upper Halls | Akhtang | Steals blades | Aegis; four-round Feint kill |
| Upper Halls | Spirit of Darkmoor | Mass Scald if bladed | No blades; mass traps only |
| Upper Halls | Shane von Shane | Heal triggers a 1,000 Vampire; needs a hit every round | Shield before heals; keep hitting |
| Graveyard | Yevgenny | Stuns bladers; round 5 kills low-health wizards | Four-round Feint kill; stay healthy |
| Graveyard | Shane von Shane (Storm) | Free Supercharge if bladed at high pips | Traps; blade only when his pips are low |
| Graveyard | Malistaire the Undying | Steals universal blades and shields; removes universal traps | Aegis, stacked Mass Feints, Doom and Gloom on the kill round |

Extra drops worth having: school damage **athames** (Graveyard) and a
**ring** from Aphrodite (gold key boss in Castle Darkmoor).

**Unknown:** gold key drop rate per run. Log keys per full clear for the first
few runs before planning long sessions.

## Step 5: Spend keys on Ship of Fools

Drowned Dan, Celestia Science Center. Level 66 to 109 is Tier 3; 110+ is Tier 4.

1. Kill his minions **one at a time with single-target hits only**.
2. Each minion death reveals the next digit of a four-digit code. **Each digit
   is a pip cost.**
3. With minions dead, cast four spells matching the code's pip costs, in
   order. That removes his aura.
4. Keep single-target spells at each pip cost ready (Dark Sprite 1, Ghoul 2,
   Vampire 4, Skeletal Pirate, Crow Horseman, Wraith). Keep hit-all spells out
   until the code is done.

Stop at 35 Ship of Fools Spellements.
