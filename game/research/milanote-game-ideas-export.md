That being said, it's still essential to create something fresh and unique that players will enjoy. Here are a few ideas on how you can modify or expand upon existing CCG (Collectible Card Game) mechanics to make your game stand out:

1. **Terrain and Positioning**: Unlike many CCGs, physical board games often take advantage of positioning mechanics. Integrating a 'board' or 'map' into your game, where players can move their cards around, might provide an extra layer of strategy. Each card could have different stats or abilities based on their position.
2. **Dynamic Resource System**: Most card games have a mana or energy system to play cards. You could innovate by making a resource system that isn't linear. Perhaps resources change based on the phase of the game, the cards in play, or player choices.
3. **Deck Customization**: Players could have the ability to customize not only their deck but also the properties of individual cards. This could involve modifying a card's attack, health, abilities, or even creating completely new cards.
4. **Synergy Mechanic**: Create more pronounced 'synergy' mechanics. In some games, certain cards work better together based on their attributes or classes. You can emphasize this aspect by designing your game in a way that encourages smart combinations and synergy.
5. **Changing Win Conditions**: Instead of simply reducing your opponent's life to zero, you could incorporate different winning conditions. Maybe there are specific 'objective' cards that need to be captured or defended, or players have to complete certain tasks to win.
6. **Narrative Elements**: Incorporating a story or campaign mode can significantly change the feel of the game. Each game could unfold as part of a larger narrative, with choices made during gameplay impacting the story's outcome.
7. **Card Evolution**: Cards could change and evolve as they're used, gaining new abilities or attributes. This could add a layer of long-term strategy, as players have to consider not just the current game, but also how their cards might evolve over time.

Remember, the key is to build upon existing mechanics and put your unique twist on them. Not only will this help you avoid legal issues, but it will also make your game more exciting and attractive to players.
