# Native Godot battle parity

## Current playtest baseline: 30 / 2 / 10 / 30

September 20 update: Adam superseded the five-card active-match plan. Both practice and island encounters now start with **30 health each**, **30 physical cards per deck**, **2-mana hero powers** and **10 maximum mana**. Generated Coin is extra. Both sides can use a power; the opponent currently shares the player's class power.

Launch `Play Eyeland.cmd`, then choose **Play** in Collection. The saved custom deck uses any of the 100 prototype cards, with at most two copies of ordinary cards and one of each Legendary. Click an entry in the deck to remove a copy; use Add under a catalog card to replace it. Incomplete decks cannot start. Starter presets and Play again provide quick matches. Original earned ownership and its legacy five-card save field remain preserved separately; that field no longer drives matches.

**F8 / Report bug** writes local diagnostics in the Godot user directory's `playtest-reports/`: seed, deck, current battle state, recent actions and exact card definitions. Tell Codex what happened and what you expected. This is a snapshot, not a complete replay or upload.
