# Card illustration and interface pass, September 21

All 100 catalog cards now have distinct original generated illustrations. Four five-by-five atlases are mapped in `manifest.json`; it also records exact generation prompts and ordered card IDs. The built-in image-generation tool produced the paintings. Originals are retained in Codex generated_images, and the selected files are copied into this project without bitmap editing. The nine-cell starter atlas remains a fallback only. The Coin retains its original SVG. These are AI-generated prototype assets, not commissioned human paintings.

The visual reference remains Hearthstone's readable card hierarchy and portrait tokens, with Eyeland's own teal, parchment, amber and natural-material art direction. Creature species and spell subjects match their card names. Full-card illustrations are center-cropped without distortion; board tokens and previews use the same canonical artwork. Artifact provenance stays in documents, not player-facing tooltips.

Interface changes: original illustrated tabletop; hero portraits in class selection and practice results; restrained element accents; long token names fit their banners; deck rows show thumbnails; consistent search/filter styling and focus rings; filter reset; disabled Add explanations; clear page navigation tooltips.

Semantic colors retained: background #102833, panels #183541, primary #e6be77, text #f6eddc, secondary text #a9bec1, playable/friendly highlight #77d2bd. Poppins for functional text, Cormorant Garamond for titles. No web framework introduced into Godot.

Verification: 309 art/layout checks cover 100 non-null distinct atlas regions, valid crops, Coin art, and collection widths at 1280x800/1024x720. Initial width assertions mixed physical and stretched coordinates; corrected to the logical viewport after screenshot inspection. Full-hand hover initially passed 39/40 with a timing miss, then 40/40 on rerun; collection 466/466, constructed 26/26, draw journey 9/9 and friendly targeting 12/12 pass. Screenshots of setup, elemental collections, battle and practice result were inspected. This is a substantial art and usability pass, not proof of universally perfect UX or an unaided human playtest.
