# Cards and collection

## Direction

Cards are literal capture vessels for **minions and spells**. Creatures are themselves playable cards, superseding the early brainstorm in which each creature generated a separate move deck. Recruitable party allies with mini-decks are a distinct later concept.

Common / Rare / Epic / Legendary are the four rarity tiers. At full size the deck is roughly 30 cards, with at most two copies of ordinary cards and one of each Legendary. Start with five physical deck slots, not five types repeated into a hidden larger deck. One is a Legendary companion; four support it. A library holds owned cards and enables customization.

Defeating creatures already awards their own cards and resources in the existing expedition. Capture as a separate action remains unresolved: victory reward does not prove a capture mechanic exists. Bosses may additionally drop powerful RNG loot. Packs are associated with island/world content and bought with in-game gold; tradability can vary by pack/reward provenance.

## Starter evolution

Three-, six- and nine-mana stages were Adam's example. Usage, achievements and boss milestones were considered as triggers. Decide whether evolution adds another card, replaces a definition, or grants an alternate form. Preserve attachment and meaningful deck costs; do not automatically make every starter into the strongest stat block. The starter's Legendary label needs an explicit reconciliation with low-rank fizzle.

## Data direction

Keep stable definition IDs separate from owned instances, acquisition source and trade binding. Definitions describe type, element, rarity, class, cost, stats, rules and effects. Ownership eventually tracks quantity/provenance; mastery belongs to character/card relationship, not rarity alone. Recipes consume owned instances/resources rather than modifying the global definition.

## Slice

Four element-specific starter placeholders share a simple three-mana chassis. They are original placeholder names and have no implemented evolution. Supporting cards teach protection, healing, damage and minion combat. The starter uses an isolated content catalog so prototype-only cards do not enter legacy constructed/Discover pools. Art is replaceable through the existing card-art component.

Open: capture odds and restrictions, duplicate reward policy, library limits, spell-binding lore, collectible bosses, trading evolution/bound starters, and the exact full-deck requirement at each capacity.
