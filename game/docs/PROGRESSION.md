# Progression

**Superseding September 20 playtest decision:** Adam requested fixed 30-card decks now. The active Godot mode implements that baseline. The 5-to-30 ladder below records the earlier brainstorm; it is not the current deck-size implementation target. Companion, collection and world progression remain intended future systems.

## Direction

Begin with five cards: a starter Legendary and four companions/supporting spells. Grow deck capacity roughly **5 → 10 → 15 → 20 → 25 → 30**. The collection can exceed the active deck. Character growth, deck capacity, card ownership and mastery are related but separate concepts.

The first story arc concept is **five islands / roughly 50 levels**, with a new island around every ten levels. Later islands introduce cards, classes, mechanics, bosses and story. Cradle-style recognizable ranks make breakthroughs legible and reveal a wider scale of power.

The starter evolves into more powerful forms. Adam's example was a three-mana starter followed by six- and nine-mana forms. Names, triggers and whether old forms remain usable are open; copied Pokémon names were analogies, not content specifications.

## Hypotheses

Milestone achievements or bosses could evolve the starter; usage/XP was also considered. Ranks could unlock reliable rarity use, access and NPC recognition. Horizontal additions between major rank breakthroughs could prevent endless level inflation and obsolete collections. These were suggestions, not a locked economy of power.

Island gates might branch and later reconverge. The transcript's “unlock at level 20 after the starter” example leaves the exact first departure threshold ambiguous. Use ranges as planning labels, not hardcoded campaign law.

## Open implementation decisions

Define what awards XP, time per level/island, exact deck thresholds, maximum versus required deck size, respec/class changes, rank names, PvP normalization, returning to low-level content, and persistence migration. Boss scaling based on player progress from older research must coexist with fixed story power gaps and party scaling; these are different axes.

## Slice boundary

Five-card loadout and starter identity exist in data. No XP grind, level-50 progression, automatic deck growth, evolution or mastery rolls are implied by those fields. Legacy 12-card Ember Reach and 30-card practice remain intact.
