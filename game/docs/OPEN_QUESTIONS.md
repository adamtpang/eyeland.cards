# Open questions

## Resolve through the next playtest

1. Does a five-card deck teach the battle or hit fatigue too early? Keep normal draws, shrink opening hand, or recycle for tutorials?
2. What makes a Legendary companion feel personal beyond its rarity color? Which first ability creates a memorable decision?
3. Is a small navigable map useful between battles? Does encounter entry/recovery make sense?
4. Can a player explain the creature-card reward and choose to use it? The slice stores a reward but deck-edit integration remains a next step.

## Before full starter-island production

- Home/resource names, key relationships, pirate motive/leader, threat timing, survival outcome and concrete departure goal.
- Starter choice tied to class, element, region or independent selection? Race/spawn variation versus shared opening?
- Evolution through usage, quests, achievements or bosses; replace versus retain forms; example costs 3/6/9.
- Mastery ranks and fizzle table; explicit Legendary starter exception; mana/card loss and displayed odds.
- Exact deck-capacity thresholds and required size; XP sources and island departure levels.
- Guaranteed defeated-creature reward versus separate capture; boss bonus RNG and duplicate outcomes.
- Is Ember Reach a later story island or separate test expedition? How do older procedural-world ideas fit authored chapters?

## Before multiplayer/economy

- Sequential versus simultaneous turns, targeting, shared/separate boards and mana, action conflict resolution.
- Boss HP/actions/deck scaling; AI-slot weight; joining/leaving and disconnect rules.
- Revive versus ordinary heal, bench limits, substitution costs and human replacement behavior.
- Personal loot and host/individual story ownership; PvP normalization for level, gear, deck size and fizzle.
- Bound/tradable provenance, market model, recipe sacrifices, output binding, protected starters, currency ledger.
- Chat/voice scopes and controls, friends and optional guilds; trustworthy server authority.

## Longer horizon

Monarch identities/domains and binding origins; faction/bounty consequences; branching island order; post-50 ranks versus infinite levels; mount access, equipment and pets; platform/art style and eventual online architecture; business model. No decision in this set authorizes real-money sales or publication.

## Settled direction should not be reopened accidentally

Use eyeland.cards spelling; Hearthstone-style minion/spell battles; literal card vessels; collection/starter evolution ambition; four rarity tiers and four core world elements; weak beloved home and growth to protect it; themed islands; friend/co-op ambition. Numerical examples, proposed names and assistant suggestions are not equally settled.
