# World and story

## Direction

Home is a weak, cozy island with family and friends the player knows. Its people grow or produce a valuable resource. Pirates arrive with overwhelmingly greater power, making the gap between local life and the wider world concrete. The player needs to leave, grow and find a way to protect home from the next attack.

Tease the major threat very early, as Wizard101 and Cradle inspired Adam to do. Let the player witness or participate in the power-gap moment. Their reason to grow comes from relationships and vulnerability, not an abstract level target.

Cards are literal vessels that capture creatures and spells. This ties battle, collection, loot, crafting and trade to the world's logic. Each element has an associated mythic being or supremely powerful monarch; domains have their own geography, culture and rules. One Piece-style pirates, navy-like order and other factions can disagree over resources, knowledge and power. Bounties communicate notoriety and consequences.

## Story proposal, not canon

Home chores reveal why a magical crop matters to ordinary families. A small creature encounter teaches binding. A distant sail or envoy hints at elemental politics. The raid overwhelms the local champion. Someone survives to describe the next threat; leaving becomes an active choice to protect others. A later return should show what the player can now accomplish.

The exact crop, home name, family members, pirate leader, invasion timing, survival outcome and departure condition are not decided. A monarch intervening to save everyone was an assistant suggestion; it is not required. Neither forced family deaths nor a literal countdown was established.

## Mythic questions

Who invented binding? Are creatures consenting companions, contracted beings or prisoners? How is a spell captured? Are monarchs humans, beasts or both? Do four elements imply four rulers or many competing claimants? What is the long-term mystery connecting islands? Reveal answers through quests and environmental hints, not an opening encyclopedia.

## Slice choices

“Home Island,” a relative, a friend, a resin crop and distant pirate sails are replaceable placeholders. A friendly mythic rumor and a protected dock communicate scale without pretending the invasion or five-island arc is already playable.
