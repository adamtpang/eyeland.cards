# Play the Godot MVP

Godot is the current engine. Double-click **Play Eyeland.cmd** at the repository root.

WASD/arrows walk, Shift runs, Space jumps, right-drag orbits the camera, wheel zooms, and E interacts nearby. Your character and companion are visible in a physical 3D island.

Choose your class and element, visit Family home, head east and battle the Resin Crab. Win its card, swap it into your five-card deck, rest at camp and try a rematch. Progress survives closing the game. Quitting mid-battle returns you to camp at one HP on reopening.

Full controls, launch requirements, verification and limitations: [Godot README](../godot/README.md).

This is a verified small solo vertical slice, with an automated rendered playthrough. Human enjoyment has not yet been tested. Crafting, evolution, multiplayer and the full island episode remain planned.
