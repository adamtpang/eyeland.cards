# Game vision

## Direction

The spelling is **eyeland.cards**: E-Y-E-L-A-N-D dot cards. An adventurous card-combat RPG across themed islands, with a long-term shared-world/MMORPG ambition. Start small and weak, care about home, collect creatures and spells in literal cards, become capable of reaching more dangerous places, and eventually protect the people left behind. Solo progression should be worthwhile; friends make adventures richer.

The emotional spine is discovery plus earned power: “I found this creature, made its card part of my deck, and can now reach somewhere I couldn't before.” The starter legendary is a companion with a future, not disposable tutorial loot. The world should reveal an immense gap between village life and mythic power early.

## Inspiration map

| Inspiration | Desired contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Hearthstone | Core minion/spell combat, mana, deck construction, readable interactions, four rarities |
| Pokémon | Collection, starter attachment/evolution, loadouts, allies and substitution |
| Wizard101 | Quests across worlds, card battles in an explorable setting, small-party co-op, recipe NPCs |
| Minecraft | Resources and learned recipes yielding things the player wants |
| FIFA | Tradable/untradable ownership, player market, sacrificing collected cards through SBC-like recipes |
| Breath of the Wild | Exploration and growth opening new places, traversal goals, recovery pacing |
| Cradle | Clear power ladder, a weak home, earned breakthroughs and expanding world scale |
| Avatar | Four-element clarity and mythic beings behind everyday elemental magic |
| One Piece | Distinct islands, factions, powerful figures, bounties and a cohesive wider mystery |
| Overwatch | Tank/support/damage contributions and satisfying team combinations |

These are design intentions, not a request to reproduce their characters, assets or complete feature sets. D&D/Lord of the Rings supply class-fantasy language; Skylanders was another collection reference. Greek mythology supplied the analogy of rulers with distinct domains. WoW, FFXIV, GW2, OSRS and EVE appeared as study suggestions, not additional locked systems. Sword Art Online was discussed but no trapped-player/death-game premise was adopted.

## Working principles

Keep combat legible and let simple cards interact. Exploration must feed deck decisions. Power should open possibilities, not only inflate numbers. Show the world's scale without requiring a giant first release. Friendship is a product goal; mandatory grouping is not established. Test the smallest loop before treating a technical build as a fun game.

## Open

Final art style, camera, audience/platform priorities, world geometry, monetization and the eventual shared-world architecture are unresolved. The current web engine is the implementation baseline, not a final statement that native clients can never exist.
