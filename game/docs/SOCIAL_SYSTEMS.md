# Social systems

## Direction

Players should be able to make friends inside the game. Text chat, party voice and friends are desired social tools. Team roles, help with tough encounters, recruiting allies and tavern PvP/trading give people reasons to meet. Solo exploration remains meaningful.

## Hypotheses

Taverns can be social centers with friendly duels, recruitment and trade. Emotes, quick chat, recurring encounters and small acts of help can make relationships grow naturally. Optional party/guild voice is preferable to default global voice as a working proposal. Guilds, spectators, ranked tables and automated matchmaking are not settled V1 requirements.

## Before online implementation

Specify invites, acceptance, mute/block/report, party permissions, text scope, voice consent, friend presence and disconnect behavior. Decide who owns quest progress and what happens when friends are at different stages. These are necessary product design tasks for an eventual online system, not features the local prototype claims to provide.

## Slice boundary

Home NPCs are authored dialogue, not real people or simulated users. There is no chat service, account system, voice connection, external communication or public server in this work. Existing duel AI emotes remain local game feedback.
