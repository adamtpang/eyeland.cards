# Co-op and PvP

## Direction

Play alone or with friends; optional PvP fits taverns. Small-party **1–4 player** co-op is the target concept, not a completed architecture. A player has about **30 HP**. Up to three AI helpers can occupy party slots, with roughly **10 HP** and **ten-card mini-decks** each. A human joins by replacing an AI slot and bringing their own decisions/deck.

Revive/heal cards and Pokémon-like substitution from a bench should create rescue and adaptation decisions. Whether ordinary healing revives a fallen ally or only explicit revive cards do so remains open. Allies are participants, not ordinary minions on the seven-creature board.

## Boss scaling — unresolved

Adam suggested a 30-HP solo boss becoming 60 HP for two full players, and possibly doubled decks or turns. The assistant proposed extra actions/mechanics with more moderate HP to avoid long fights. Neither supersedes the other as a final formula. Test solo, solo+AI, duo and full party separately; helpers are weaker than humans, so counting every slot as a full player would distort tuning.

Joining mid-battle needs a rule for scaling, turn boundary, deck/HP replacement, reconnects and reward eligibility. Freeze those rules before implementing networking. A later world-boss model with multiple independent squads and shared global HP was only an assistant suggestion.

## Turn-order experiments

Compare sequential players, rotating initiative and simultaneous planning with ordered resolution. Measure wait time, readable targeting, conflicting actions, shared mana versus separate mana and whether support players retain agency. Target under-ten-minute early fights was an assistant suggestion, not validated pacing.

## PvP proposals

Tavern duels begin plausibly at 1v1; 2v2/3v3 team modes, spectators, ranks and seasons are possibilities. Decide normalization, mastery/fizzle policy, gear and deck-capacity fairness before competitive play. Personal PvE loot, optional grouping and host/individual quest progress were suggested solutions, not implemented networking.

## Current boundary

The slice is local solo versus the existing AI. No fake connected players, live chat, market or co-op controls are presented as working.
