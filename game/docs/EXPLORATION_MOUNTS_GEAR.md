# Exploration, mounts and gear

## Direction

Growth makes dangerous areas reachable, and those areas contain cards that enable further growth. New islands should feel like discovery, not merely a higher-level menu. Land, sea and sky mounts expand travel; underwater and underground mounts were also requested. Ships may connect islands. Revisited places can reveal new opportunities when traversal improves.

Weapons and armor feed into card combat: starting weapons, armor or special cards/spells were contemplated. Keep their battle impact visible through existing combat concepts rather than an unrelated invisible stat system. A weapon adding two cards or armor giving five starting armor were assistant examples, not settled balance.

Rest at campfires/inns after fighting to restore health and mark session boundaries. Exact short-rest versus full-rest rules, costs and safe logout behavior remain open. Recovery should support pacing without excessive backtracking.

## Pets hypothesis

Pets might provide small utility or passive bonuses; mounts focus on access. That separation was suggested, not finalized. Do not confuse pets, card minions, evolving starters and recruitable AI party members; their identity overlap needs design.

## Slice choices

Use a small top-down grid with keyboard and on-screen movement, clear land/water boundaries, home/field/encounter/campfire/dock landmarks and contextual interaction. Placeholder symbols and terrain colors keep content editable. Battle health carries back to this slice; resting restores it. The dock hints at future travel but does not pretend to load an unfinished island.

## Open

Camera and final movement, free-roaming versus paths, jumping/climbing, hazards, encounter initiation, fast travel, traversal gates, mount acquisition, equipment slots, durability, loot loss on defeat and whether injury persists across story checkpoints. The current slice is an experiment in flow, not a final world renderer.
