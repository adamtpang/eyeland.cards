# Tinkerer

**Verb: build your own.**

> Not an SRD class. Adam's own, from the Milanote export: 'engineer, can craft mounts and mechsuits for battle'.

One verb per class, per [`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) principle 4. A class you
cannot describe in one sentence will not read at the table.

## What a Tinkerer deck also gets

Every deck may also play the cards in
[**the common collection**](neutral.md). Those are the shared floor this class
is measured against: if a Tinkerer card is not better than a neutral card at
the same cost *for a Tinkerer plan*, it has not earned its slot.

## Deck rule

A legal deck is **one class plus neutrals**. You may not mix two classes. That
is what makes the verb above mean anything: choosing Tinkerer is choosing to
be good at "build your own" and to give up every other class's answer to it.

## Status

Designed, **not yet playtested**. The numbers here are first drafts and should
be expected to move once a real deck meets the AI simulator or a human.
