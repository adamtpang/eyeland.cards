# Wizard

**Verb: spells pay off spells.**

One verb per class, per [`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) principle 4. A class you
cannot describe in one sentence will not read at the table.

## What a Wizard deck also gets

Every deck may also play the cards in
[**the common collection**](neutral.md). Those are the shared floor this class
is measured against: if a Wizard card is not better than a neutral card at
the same cost *for a Wizard plan*, it has not earned its slot.

## Deck rule

A legal deck is **one class plus neutrals**. You may not mix two classes. That
is what makes the verb above mean anything: choosing Wizard is choosing to
be good at "spells pay off spells" and to give up every other class's answer to it.

## Status
