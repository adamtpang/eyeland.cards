# Adam's game taste

Updated 2026-08-29. This is the recommendation memory for games Adam has
played, loved, or expressed interest in. It separates favorites from games
that are merely owned so future recommendations do not overfit a Steam
library screenshot.

## What Adam is looking for now

- Games with other people, especially shared worlds, public matchmaking, or
  communities where playing repeatedly can turn strangers into friends.
- Meaningful progression and customization: decks, parties, classes, gear,
  weapons, towers, machines, or distinct characters.
- A satisfying mix of building, exploration, combat, and boss or quest goals.
- Low friction: free or inexpensive to try, quick to start, and still fun
  without an existing friend group.

## Core favorites and why

- **Minecraft, especially FTB/modded:** a malleable world, redstone logic,
  machines, automation, mods, and co-op memories. "Lego with zombies."
- **Terraria:** co-op boss progression, new weapons, classes, RPG progression,
  and the combination of building and fighting.
- **Wizard101:** a social fantasy world, schools, memorable spells, and
  strategic card combat.
- **Pokemon:** party customization, move customization, battle strategy, and
  an explorable adventure.
- **Hearthstone:** deckbuilding and live tactical decisions.
- **Overwatch:** fast role-based team play and distinct heroes.
- **Grand Theft Auto V:** a shared sandbox and chaotic play with friends.
- **Rainbow Six Siege:** tactical team play with friends.
- **Call of Duty / Black Ops 2:** immediate action with friends.
- **Halo 4:** co-op campaign.
- **Rock Band / Guitar Hero:** songs and mastery.
- **Bloons:** interesting towers and new strategic tools.
- **FIFA / EA Sports FC:** connection to the real sport and "body chess."

## Recommendation rubric

Prefer games that:

1. Are free or easy to try.
2. Have public matchmaking, shared-world encounters, guilds, or welcoming
   community systems rather than requiring an existing group.
3. Offer a satisfying solo fallback.
4. Provide real build, deck, party, class, gear, or world customization.
5. Create visible goals such as bosses, quests, unlocks, ranks, or projects.
