About the project

Build the duel before the world.

eyeland.cards is an open-world card-combat MMORPG in development. Its design joins tactical card duels, collectible spellbooks, creature companions, and exploration across a floating archipelago called the Eyeland.

The project follows 5 explicit stages: v0 Duel, v1 Deck, v2 Island, v3 World, and v4 Online. A playable terminal prototype currently exercises turns, hands, pips, creatures, spell resolution, and win states. The first public itch.io build has not shipped yet.

Who operates the site

eyeland.cards is an Anchor Marianas project created by Adam Pangelinan. Adam builds the game and publishes this site; his broader portfolio is available at adampang.com, and the public source repository is available on GitHub.

What visitors can do today

The site documents the game direction, shows 3 example starter cards, and offers a free email waitlist for the future itch.io link. There is no account, public download, payment, or paid plan today.
