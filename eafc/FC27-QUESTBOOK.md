### How to do each one fast

**Manager Live (1,000 SP, 3 challenges)**
- *Transfer Savvy* (~5 min): pick Barcelona, accept every incoming offer until you show a profit.
- *Winning Start* (~10 min): PSG, Beginner, 3-minute halves. Score twice each match, then sim to full time. 3 wins before 13 September in-game, no losses.
- *Top at Christmas* (~10 min): PSG, default settings. Sim straight to Christmas from the calendar, not day by day. Play a match yourself only if the lead narrows. Lower board expectations if offered.
- Each challenge is its own save. If one won't complete, re-read its exact goal on the challenge card.

**Manager Career (1,200 SP, 5 full matches, one save)**
- Beginner difficulty, 3-minute halves.
- Full matches only: simulated matches don't count. Result doesn't matter.
- All 5 on the same save. Skip cutscenes, press conferences, transfers and training between games.

**Player Career (1,250 SP, 10 match objectives)**
- Endrick at Real Madrid, matches on Highlights mode.
- Pause at kickoff, read the 3 match objectives, finish all 3 each game.
- Objectives sometimes fail to register: plan for 5 matches, not 4.

**The Grounds (1,000 SP weekly + 100 daily)**
- First confirm in the FC Objectives tab that these pay Season Points: early reports said some paid coins.
- Drop Kick targets first: solo, no queue, and the weekly 50 also covers the daily 10.
- Let daily matches and goals count toward the weekly Kickabout and Rush totals.
- Rush and Kickabout need other players, so queue at busy times. Lowest SP per minute; finish the weekly only if time is left.
