Privacy policy

Effective August 30, 2026

eyeland.cards is operated by Adam Pangelinan for Anchor Marianas. This policy describes the current static website and its email-based waitlist; the site has no user accounts, checkout, analytics, advertising trackers, or website database.

Information you choose to send

The waitlist form runs in your browser and opens a pre-filled message in your own email application. eyeland.cards does not transmit or store the entered address unless you choose to send that email. A sent message is handled by your email provider and the operator's email provider.

Email received through the waitlist is used to identify requests for the future itch.io build link and related development notes. Direct messages may also be used to answer the question or request described in the message.

Hosting and automatic data

Vercel hosts this website and may process ordinary request information needed to deliver and secure it, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser user agent, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. Vercel's own privacy terms govern that processing.

Cookies, analytics, and third parties

The current site does not set cookies or load analytics, advertising, social pixels, or third-party scripts. Links to itch.io, GitHub, adampang.com, or an email application leave this site and are governed by those services' policies.

Your choices

Visitors can use the public site without sending an email. To ask about a message you sent, request deletion from the operator's mailbox, or raise a privacy concern, contact adamtpang@gmail.com.

This policy will be updated if eyeland.cards adds a hosted signup form, analytics, accounts, payments, or other data collection. The effective date above identifies the current version.
